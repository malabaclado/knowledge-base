## Highlights

> As a doctor and scientist, I see a lot of conflict about the eating habits of thin people. [⤴️](https://omnivore.app/me/https-healthinsider-news-what-actually-makes-naturally-thin-peop-18917bc9440#11e6c7ba-58b2-4e13-81fb-ac09b8c0816f) 

> **Answer 1:** Our slim friends sometimes do consume fewer calories throughout the day. They will have pizza for lunch but will skip dinner, for example. 
> 
> They might also be more active, not necessarily by going to the gym daily, but by walking more, being fidgety, parking a car further from the shop… But it’s just a small number of them. [⤴️](https://omnivore.app/me/https-healthinsider-news-what-actually-makes-naturally-thin-peop-18917bc9440#7986396f-a9bf-42ce-8d26-e7ed1ea7e979) 

> Thin people have an active enzyme that regulates metabolism and is often not activated (although there are ways to “fire it up”) in overweight and obese people.
> 
> It’s called an AMPK ([5′ AMP-activated protein kinase](https://selfhacked.com/blog/natural-ampk-activators/)), an energy sensor within our bodies. [⤴️](https://omnivore.app/me/https-healthinsider-news-what-actually-makes-naturally-thin-peop-18917bc9440#8950e974-83ca-41f2-b6ae-7326ab51d889) 

> To activate the AMPK through intermittent fasting, a person should never decrease the amount of food drastically. 
> 
> Instead, they should follow a personalized fasting approach. It also eliminates worries like tracking, looking for healthy recipes, calculating, and many others. [⤴️](https://omnivore.app/me/https-healthinsider-news-what-actually-makes-naturally-thin-peop-18917bc9440#9788456f-8499-42bd-9c65-5fba2ee45fa5) 

> Yet exercise is widely accepted as one of the best ways to improve health and activate the AMPK. [⤴️](https://omnivore.app/me/https-healthinsider-news-what-actually-makes-naturally-thin-peop-18917bc9440#b52521ad-0796-4a65-9b7c-bce613ff98e8) 

> Ice baths have been shown to activate AMPK in tests with rodents. No studies on humans have been done yet, but considering how similarly we react to stimulus, this might also have the same effect on people.
