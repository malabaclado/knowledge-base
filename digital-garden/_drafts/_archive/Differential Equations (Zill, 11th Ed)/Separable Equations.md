**Separable equations** are differential equations of the form:
$$\frac{dy}{dx} = g(x)h(y)$$

Method of solution:
- Separate the variables into $\frac{dy}{h(y)} = g(x)dx$
- Integrate both sides

---

###### Examples

---

1. Solve $$(1+x)dy - y dx = 0$$

---
2. Solve the initial-value problem $$\frac{dy}{dx} = -\frac{x}{y}, \quad y(4)=3$$

---
3. Solve $$\frac{dy}{dx} = y^2 -4 $$

---
4. Solve the initial-value problem $$(e^{2y}-y) cos (x) \frac{dy}{dx} = e^y sin (2x), \quad y(0) = 0$$

---
5. Solve the initial value problem $$\frac{dy}{dx} = e^{-x^2}, \quad y(3) = 5$$
