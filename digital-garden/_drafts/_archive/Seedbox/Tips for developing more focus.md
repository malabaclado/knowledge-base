# Tips for developing more focus
1. Declutter. 
	1. Clean physical space. Clean your desk. 
	2. Organize your notes.
	3. Mental declutter. Visualize your goals. Know your priorities. 
2. Avoid Internet and Social Networking Sites 
	1. Log out SNS from your PC browser 
	2. Use mute notifications and flip your phone down.
	3. If you work on phone, use focus mode.
	4. Use bedtime mode when about to sleep.
3. Work full-screen
	1. Use apps on full screen. 
	2. Use multiple desktops feature wisely.
