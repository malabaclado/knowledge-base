# Talking to Prospects
Things that you would need to know:
- Work/Career
	- What do you do in life?
- Family. 
	- Do you have kids? Are you married? 
- Date of birth/age 
- Dependents 
- Health status 
- Existing coverage 
- Lifestyle & Hobbies 
- Financial Dreams/Goals 
- General Dreams/Goals
