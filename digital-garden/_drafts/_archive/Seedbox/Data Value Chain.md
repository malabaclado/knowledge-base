> Analytics mines and refines data to extract its value as it transforms data to information, to insights and to imperatives.

4. What could be done next? (Imperatives)

# Data Value in Retail - An Example
- (Data) Whenever we go shopping, data is recorded such as time and location, order record and price. This might be stored on receipts, on an ecommerce website or on an inventory list. 
- (Information) The shop owner can consolidate the data to know what happened. This gives them information on how much sales they make, which products sell the most and which time sell the most.
- (Insight) The shop owner might wonder, why did it happen and what could happen next? They might discover what makes a product sell more or sell less.
- (Imperative) From the insights, the shop owner take actions that could improve the business.
