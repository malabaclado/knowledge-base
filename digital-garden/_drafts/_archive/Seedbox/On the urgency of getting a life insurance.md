# On the urgency of getting a life insurance
**Step 1 Notes: Deciding on your persuasive speech topic**

After reflecting on the guide questions in selecting a topic (Beebe & Beebe, 2016), provide your responses to the following:

1.  What’s your final topic? - Getting a life insurance at a young age is beneficial. 
2.  Why are you stirred by this issue? - I am a practicing financial advisor and a sales insurance agent. I want to insure people's lives and help them manage their finances in order to achieve their goals.
3.  Why is it important to engage in discourse regarding this issue? - A lot of Filipinos undervalue insurance products. 
4.  What is your personal stand on this issue? - Life insurance is beter purchased when young. 
5.  What do you think are your audience’s thoughts on this issue? - Audiences does not care about life insurance at the moment. They might think about getting it at a later age, or get it from employment.
