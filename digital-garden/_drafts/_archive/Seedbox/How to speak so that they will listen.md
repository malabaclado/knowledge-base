# How to speak so that they will listen

## 4 Types of Listeners
1. Auditory
2. Visual
3. Kinesthetic
4. Digital

## 3 Necessary Skills a Speaker Should Have
Tindig - Your looks, dress, posture
Tinig - Proper voice, tune, intonation
Titig - eye contact ➡ makes you more credible and honest


## 7 Tips How to Speak so that they will Listen
1. People listen to their "friends"

>   "I do not care how much you know until I know how much you care"


> "Who do you say that I am?"
  
2. People listen to "vision"
3. Allow them to see the bigger picture
  
  Credibility = Competence + Character
  
4. Be accountable and responsible
5. People listens to inspirations
- Share 
  ✅ Beautiful stories
  ✅ Success stories
  
6. Remind that life is short
7. Remind people to be grateful 

## 4 Things you need as a preacher
1. Stay focused

 > "You cannot feed everybody. But if you can feed one, there will be one less hungry person in the world"

Don't ever make it your goal to change someone's heart. Our goal is not to make better than others. The goal is to make ourselves better today than yesterday.

2. Know your priorities.

Every vocation has its own priorities. 

> You must pray while you work, you must work while you pray

3. Live a balanced life.

4. You must not be afraid to make painful decisions for God.
