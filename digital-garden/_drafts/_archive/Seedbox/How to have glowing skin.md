# How to have glowing skin
- [x] Skin Care Routine
	- Try to avoid harsh chemicals, sulfates, parabens, phthalates, dyes and fragrances.
	- Cleanse Twice A Day
	- Exfoliate Your Skin Once A Week 
	- Use A Toner 
		- Your skin’s natural pH is around 5-6 (slightly acidic). Cleansers and scrubs tend to make it slightly alkaline.
		- Toner is a skin care product aimed at balancing your skin’s pH. 
	- Apply A Sunscreen
- [ ] Flex Your Muscles
	- Yoga, gym, Zumba, aerobics...whatever interests you, just go for it. Exercise helps you sweat it out and releases toxins from your body and skin.
	- Make sure you cleanse your face or take a shower post workout.
	- Stress is one of the causes of a lot of skin concerns. Stress can lead to breakouts, fine lines and wrinkles. Keep stress at bay by practicing meditation and mindfulness.
- [ ] Eat Right And Healthy
	- Tomato, Walnuts, Avocado, Berries, Fish, Lemon, Orange, Sweet potato
- [x] Home Remedies For Glowing Skin
- [ ] Stay Hydrated
	- Drink 2-3 liters of water every day and see what wonders it can do to your skin.
- [ ] Sound Sleep
	- Remember to get 7-8 hours of sound sleep for glowing skin.
- [x] Quit Smoking And Alcohol 
- [x] Shorten Your Shower
