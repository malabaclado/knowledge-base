# Acing the Data Science Resume

Speaker: Jen Bricker, Head of Career Services at DataCamp
- It is best to represent your data skills and business skills. 

### Beat the Applicant Tracking System
- 99% of fortune companies depend on Applicant Tracking Systems.
- ATS filter out candidates who don't meet the criteria.

![[Pasted image 20220623225146.png]]

- Don't put charts, images that may easily corrupt. 
- Put hyperlinks instead of long urls
- Don't quantify/qualify your skills.

### Organizing your resume: Put your strongest assets first. 

Ask yourself: Why are you best for this position?

![[Pasted image 20220623225524.png]]

- When you passed the ATS and a human looked at your resume, you need to hit them first and hit them really hard. 
- Make your resume really short. Instead, put all your achievements and history in your LinkedIn profile.

Sample: Career Transition Resume 
![[Pasted image 20220623225818.png]]

Sample: Recent Graduate Resume 
![[Pasted image 20220623230158.png]]

- Focus on your skills. Focus on your portfolio. It shows that you know your stuff. 
- Next on the importance list are the certifications. 
- Follows are some work experience, or internships. Just to show that you have some sort of professionalism.

### Customized Cover Letters and Resume

**Customize your resume to every job you apply to**
![[Pasted image 20220623230856.png]]

- Focus on the company, what they're looking for and how you can help them look for the right candidate.
- There are lots of technology in Data Science. Curate the technical section only  to what is really relevant on the particular job position.
