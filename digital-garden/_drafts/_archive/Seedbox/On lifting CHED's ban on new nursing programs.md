# On lifting CHED's ban on new nursing programs
**Step 1 Notes: Deciding on your persuasive speech topic**

After reflecting on the guide questions in selecting a topic (Beebe & Beebe, 2016), provide your responses to the following:

1.  What’s your final topic? - Lifting CHED's ban on new nursing programs. 
2.  Why are you stirred by this issue? - I live in the City of Dasmarinas. Also, we are still in the pandemic where more nursing graduates are needed. 
3.  Why is it important to engage in discourse regarding this issue? - Because this is an issue that matters on on the national level. Taking part in this will help so that the authorities, mainly in CHED will listen. 
4.  What is your personal stand on this issue? -I think as of now, it makes sense to lift the ban on creating new nursing programs 
5.  What do you think are your audience’s thoughts on this issue? - I think most audience are on the latitude of non-commitment on this topic. Though I think a number of people expresses the same sentiment.
