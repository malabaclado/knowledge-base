# Module 1 - Fundamentals of Speech Communication

-   How do the elements or components of communication influence each other?   
    
-   How can you explain either ease in communication or miscommunication using the components and principles of communication?   
    
-   Why is there a need to have a comprehensive understanding of the elements and principles of communication, in order to become an effective persuasive speaker?

Communication is defined as :
> “**a transactional process** in which people **generate meaning** through the exchange of **verbal and nonverbal messages** in specific contexts, influenced by individual and societal forces and embedded in culture” (Alberts, Nakayama, & Martin, 2012, p. 9).

Communication involves the following components: 
1. message creation; 
2. meaning creation; 
3. setting; 
4. participants (individual experiences, hierarchies in relationships, culture); 
5. channels; 
6. noise; 
7. feedback; and 
8. contexts.

Levels of Communication
1. intrapersonal (self), 
2. interpersonal (dyad and small group), 
3. organizational, and 
4. public
5.(newly added ) technology-mediated and new media communication

Communication may be linear or transactional. 

Linear 👉 one-way, no feedback
Transactional 👉 
1. each communicator is a sender and receiver at the same time, 
2. meaning is created as people communicate together, 
3. communication is an ongoing process, and 
4. previous communication events and relationships influence its meaning”


> The Synergetic Model is a transactional model that, like most previous models, depicts communication as occurring when two or more people create meaning as they respond to each other and their environment. In addition, it is based on a belief in the important roles of individual and societal forces, contexts, and culture in the communication process. We discuss each of these topics in detail below, and to help clarify the the concepts, we revisit Adela’s interaction with her father once again to illustrate how they function during the communication process.\

**SYNERGETIC MODELS**
1. Individual Forces
	1. Identity
	2. Demographics: Age, race, ethnicity, gender, 
	3. Field of experience: education and experience
2. Societal Forces
	1. political, historical, economic, and social structures of a society
3. Cultural Forces

Principles of Communication
-   It is purposeful.
-   It is transactional.
-   It is a package of signals.
-   It is a process of adjustment.
-   It involves content and relationship dimensions.
-   It is ambiguous.
-   It is punctuated.
-   It is inevitable, irreversible, and unrepeatable
