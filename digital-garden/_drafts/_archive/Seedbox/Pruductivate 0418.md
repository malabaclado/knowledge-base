**How to make a brand (Where to start?)**
1. Determine your goal.
2. Define your niche. 
	1. What do you do?
	2. What are your hobbies/interests?
	3. What are your strengths?
3. Identify your target audience.
4. Choose your platform
	1. FB, Insta, Twitter, Tiktok, YT, LinkedIn 
	2. Focus on one platform. Then experiment on others.
	3. Pubmats > FB, Written content > Twitter, Videos > YouTube 
5. Personalize your brand 

Types of content 
1. Educational 
2. Entertaining 
3. Engagements 

How to attack clients on instagram 
- Post stories. Post your learnings. Document your learning journey. 
- Play the role of a teacher. 
- Post your awards. 
- Post evidence. Testimonies.


----
TIPS:
- Cross-post 
- The more you post, the more people will get to know you. 
- Dapat narerelate sa sarili yung ginagawa as a financial advisor. (How do you relate finance to your self as a Math major, Data Scientist?)
- Focus on your strengths. 
- Start with one platform, then experiment with others.

**Common Objections** 
- Wala ako budget 
	- Fo fact-finding and calculation together. Offer cash-flow analysis. 
	- Offer Pulse Products and other Term Products. 
	- Offer additional income to boost cash flow.
- Wala ako interest 
	- Why-why analysis. Uncover their reasons. Address them.
	- Guide them on the How-To's in Pulse. 
- Wala ako Time 
	- Invite for coffee / lunch or dinner / visit. 
	- Make presentations feel like its a kwentuhan or catch up 
	- If agreed to meet, be on time or better yet, arrive early.
