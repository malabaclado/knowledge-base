# On suicide
- Suicide by the life insured within the first two (2) years from the policy effectivity date or from the date of the last reinstatement, if any, shall not be compensable.
- However, suicide committed in a state of insanity shall be compensable regardless of date of commission.
- In case of suicide by the life insured within the first two (2) years from the effectivity date of any increase in sum assured, the increase in sum assured shall not be payable unless suicide was committed in a state of insanity
- Where suicide is not compensable, our liability shall be limited to the return of premiums paid
