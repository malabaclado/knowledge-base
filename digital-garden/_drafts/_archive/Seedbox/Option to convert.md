# Option to convert
At any time after the first (1st) policy year, this policy may be converted to a whole life, endowment, or investment-linked policy currently available at that time and deemed acceptable by the Company, without submitting evidence of good health, provided that: 
- the life insured has not attained the age of seventy (70); 
- the new policy shall be issued for an amount not exceeding the sum assured under this policy less any accelerated benefits already paid; 
- the sum assured of the new policy shall at least be equal to the minimum sum assured allowed by the Company at that time.

Premiums shall be according to the Company’s prevailing rate on the date of conversion and shall be computed based on the then-attained age as of last birthday of the life insured prior to the date of conversion.

If this policy is issued with an extra premium, an extra premium shall likewise be charged on the new policy. Any restrictions contained in the original policy shall be contained in the new policy.

If a supplementary benefit is to be included in the new policy, Company’s consent shall be required and the Company must be furnished with evidence of good health of the insured at the date of conversion satisfactory to the Company.

The new policy’s effectivity date shall be the date of conversion
