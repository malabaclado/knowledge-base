What it does: Prioritize your tasks based on urgency.


# Must - essential needs
- Tasks that are inevitable and non-negotiable. 
- Would be a great loss to me if I didn't do these things right now. 
- *"Goal-rewarding and urgent"*

# Should - important but not essential 
- Tasks that you should do (in line with your goals, or advantageous to your situation) but isn't critical that you should be doing it right now.
- Might be important tasks that you'd like to do in advance
- *"Goal-rewarding but not urgent"*


# Could have - nice to have 
- Not in line with your goals but doing so would bring you happiness and satisfaction
- *"Not goal-rewarding but personally-rewarding"*

# Wish - i want to but not needed right now 
- Something you'd like to do in the future. 
- Not important right now and would not bring you happiness right now. 
- Things that's you'd want to go back into when you're not doing anything else.
- *"Something I'd like to go back to"*
