
- Data Steward
	- Develop, maintain an organization's data usage and data security policies
	- Expertise in business and industry domains
	- The 'data gatekeepers' of the organization
	- Maintains data quality
	- # Related job titles
		- Data Privacy Officer
		- Data Sequrity Officer
		- Data Governance Manager
		- Data Curator
		- Data Librarian
- Data Engineers
	- Design, construct, testsand maintains data infrastructures
	- Expertise is in information tech and information science
	- Ensures data is in sync with various data sources
	- Ensures data infrastructures are available
	- # Related Job Titles
		- ETL Developer
		- Data Architect
		- Data Warehousing Professional
		- Big Data Engineer
- Data Scientist
	- Leverages statistical techniques and creates analytical models to derive new insights from data
	- Expertise in mathematics and statistics
	- Finds trends and patterns, make predictions on what could potentially happen next.
	- # Related Job Titles
		- Statistician
		- Statistical Modeler
		- Advanced Analytics Professional
- Functional Analyst
	- Uses data and derived insights to help organizations make better decisions on a specific functional domain
	- Validate the insights derived by the data scientists
	- Make the final prescription to the leaders
	- # Related Job Titles
		- Research Analyst
		- Human Resource Analyst
		- Marketing Analyst
		- Financial Analyst
		- Operations Analyst
- Analytics Manager
	- Develop and guide data projects
	- Expertise in project management
	- Brings the analytics team together and ensures the successful delivery of the data project
	- # Related Job Titles
		- Chief Data Officer
		- Project Manager
		- Data Engineering Manager
		- Data Science Manager
