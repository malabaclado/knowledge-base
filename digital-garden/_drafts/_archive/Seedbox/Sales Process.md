# Sales Process (PruOnePH)
Additional Personal Information 
- Legal Name/ Other aliases 
- Civil Status - Single, Married, Widowed, Separated, Annulled 
- Country & City of Birth
- Proof of Identity - TIN, SSS, GSIS, Passport, UMID, Driver's, PRC, Police Clearance, Postal ID, Voter's ID, Barangay Cert (with photo) 
	- Philhealth Card 
	- Senior Citizen Card 
	- OWWA ID
	- OFW ID 
	- Seaman's book 
	- Alien/Immigrant Cert 
	- Gov't Office ID 
	- NCWDP ID/Cert 
	- DSWD ID 
	- Firearms License 
	- Credit Card (with photo)
	- Health Card (with photo) 
	- School ID 
	- Birth Certificate 
	- Cert of Registration for DNFBP 


Lifestyle Info 
- Flown an aricraft (not as passenger)?
	- If yes, fill out Aviation Questionnaire Card 
- Engaged in dangerous sports or hobbies? 
	- If yes, may be required to answer Diving/Jumping Questionnaire Card or General Sports Questionnaire Card 
- Been active in politics as candidate during the last 5 days? 
- Have received threats in your life? 
- Smoked cigarettes/tobaco in the past year? 
	- How many years have you been smoking? 
	- Ave no. of sticks per day? 
- Do you consume alcohol? 
	- How much per week: 
		- Glasses of wine
		- Cans of beer 
		- Shots of liquor
- Have taken habit-forming drugs or been treated for alcoholism or drug addiction? 



Work and Pay 
- Occupation and Nature of Work/Business 
- Business Address (May be same as alient present address)
- Income 
	-  Gross Annual Income 
	- Appx. Net Worth 
	- Source of Funds 


Family Details
- Name of Mother and Father
	- Did they suffer from TB, diabetes, cancer, high blood pressure, kidney/sickle disease or mental illness? 
		- Age of diagnosis and illness
	- Are they still alive?
- Siblings
- Spouse 



Medical Information 
- Height, Weight
- Havebeen treated by a physician in the last 5 years? 
- Have had X-ray, electrocardiogram, blood studies or other diagnostic tests? 
- Have been in hospital or anything similar? 
- Have had AIDS? 
- Have had sexually transmitted diseases?(Search examples) 
- Have been tested for antibodies of AIDS?
- Have had abnormality, deformity, disease ofr disorder? 
- Been receiving treatment/medication of any kind? 

Previous Insurance 
- May previous insurance ba?
- May pending application sa ibang company?
- May in-force policy? 
- May nabago na ba (or mababago pa ba) sa mga inforce policy mo? 
- Will premiums for the insurance applied for be paid by a policy? 

Beneficiary 
- (Revocable vs Irrevocable)
- (Primary vs Secondary)



Document Checklist 
- Gov't ID (if not available, at least 2 valid non-govt ID)
