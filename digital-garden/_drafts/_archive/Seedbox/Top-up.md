# Top-up
- One-time payment which may be done anytime with the goal of increasing fund value. It provides additional insurance coverage equivalent to 125% of the top-up amount. Top-ups are subject to 3% charge. 
- Tip: Pwede mag-top-up sa ibang fund other than the fund invested in your insurance for diversification or for risk mitigation. Example: Pag down yung market sa fund mo, invest ka dun sa iba para offset yung loss.
- Top-ups require proof of insurability. 
- Top-ups follow [[Forward-pricing]]
