**Data visualization principles**
- **Every pixel in a visualization should contribute directly to the content**, emphasizing the importance of clarity and precision in data presentation.
- **Design should not overshadow content**; it should facilitate the viewer's relationship with the data.
- **Style and aesthetics cannot compensate for poor or inaccurate content**; the integrity of the data is paramount.
- Designers should account for the reader's context, biases, and assumptions when creating visualizations.
- Aesthetics and design can evoke emotional responses, making visualizations a powerful tool for changing attitudes and behaviors.
- Successful infographics tell a story and simplify complex data into a format that is easily understood.

**Audience Engagement**
- Respect the intelligence of the audience, as well-crafted visualizations can engage and inform effectively.
- Visualizations have emotional impact, making them powerful tools for changing attitudes and behaviors.
- Designers should aim to engage viewers with information and promote learning.

**Narrative and Storytelling**
- Effective data visualization involves storytelling, turning data into a coherent and engaging narrative.
- Visualizations should simplify complex information, making it accessible and digestible.
- Highlighting a single key insight can serve as the focal point, inviting viewers to explore further details.
