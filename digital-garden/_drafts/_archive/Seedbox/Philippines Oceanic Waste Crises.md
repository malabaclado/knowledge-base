# Philippines Oceanic Waste Crises

Source: [Philippines Oceanic Waste Crisis – The Organization for World Peace (theowp.org)](https://theowp.org/philippines-oceanic-waste-crisis/)
Written: April 1, 2019

---

In 2000 the [[Ecological Solid Waste Management Act]] was presented. Unfortunately, however, due to the corrupt government and directives this was not developed as desired and laws were not implemented.

“Hundreds of thousands of whales, dolphins, seals, and turtles are killed by ocean plastic pollution every year, including single-use plastics and abandoned plastic gear from the fishing industry,” said Peter Kemple, Hardy campaigner at World Animal Protection.
