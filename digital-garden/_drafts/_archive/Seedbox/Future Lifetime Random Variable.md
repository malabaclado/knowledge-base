# Future Lifetime Random Variable
- Refers to the remaining time until the death of an individual.
- It is denoted by $T_{x}$.

---
Let $(x)$ denote a person with age $x$, where $x\geq 0$. ^2ba8ef

Let $T_x=T(x)$  be the continuous random variable representing the future lifetime of a person with age $(x)$. The future lifetime is the remaining time until death. IT IS NOT the age of death.  ^36b62b
