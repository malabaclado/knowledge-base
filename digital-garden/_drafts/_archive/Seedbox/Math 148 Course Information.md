# Math 148 Course Information
## About
> The course aims to introduce to students the fundamental ideas of projective geometry, a geometry without the concept of distance and angle measure and where parallel lines meet. We deal with the incidence structureof a projective plane and discuss duality, perspectivity, and the Theorem of Desargues. Homogeneous coordinates, cross-ratio, and projective transformations are also examined.

---
## Course Outcomes
At the end of this course, the student should be able to:

1.  differentiate projective geometry from Euclidean geometry;
2.  understand what a projective plane is and learn the basic axioms of projective geometry;
3.  explain what perspectivities and projectivities are;
4.  understand the concepts of duality, harmonic sets and cross-ratio; and
5.  identify the different projective tranformations and compute images of geometric objects under  
    these transformations.
