## The Addition Principle
![[Pasted image 20220224121342.png|400]]

Remark
The Addition Principle can be extended to a finite number of evest that are **pairwise disjoint**.


Remark
Let $\mathcal{U}$ be a finite universal set and $A$, $B$ be subsets of $\mathcal{U}$.
- The Multiplication principle: $$|A\times B| = |A|\cdot|B|$$
- The Addition principle: $$|A \cup B| = |A| + |B|$$
Notation: $|A|$ denotes the cardinality (or the number of elements) of the set A.
