**Conclusion:** Cryo characters all have strong solid ideals that does not falter in the face of any hardship. 

Shenhe's ambition:( To survive the battle with the evil god, despite all the hardships.) She just wants to live, but here fate being one marked with calamity cannot be - as a survival mechanism, she developed homicidal tendencies.

Kaeya's ambition: To pay back his adopted father's kindness, and to be forgiven for all his lies. 

Rosaria's ambition: To escape from the bandits. It may as well be to know fatherly love, but it was the bandit that caused her life to ruin that she felt it. 

Eula's ambition: To break free from influence of her clan, but everyone dislikes her for being part of it. She has a difficult upbringing being part of the Lawrence clan, in addition to being judged by people due to her family's past. As a survival mechanism, she developed a habit of making threats.

Chongyun's ambition: To become the greatest exorcist in Liyue, control his abundance of yang energy, and rid the world of evil spirits.

He wants to become a "real" exorcist, which he believes fights with swords and exorcises evil, yet with his unique situation, this might seem unattainable.
