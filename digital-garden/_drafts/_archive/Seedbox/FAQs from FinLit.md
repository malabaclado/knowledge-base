# FAQs from FinLit
How much would you guys allocate to your emergency funds?
- Usually it's 3-6 months of your total monthly expenses. Best is for one year.
- Depende din talaga ang emergency fund nyo sa lifestyle nyo.

Which bank ninyo nilalagay ang emergency fund?
- Nearest accessible bank to you
