# Hospital Income

This rider serves as an income replacement.

> [!NOTE]- Positioning
> Imagine magkasakit ka and you've been hospitalized. Of course you would be covered by your sick leave. Eh what if mahospitalize ka for more than your sick leaves. Eh no work, no pay. Edi wala kang income? This rider serves as that rider. 

![[Pasted image 20220614195311.png]]
DHI provides cash benefit for each day of hospital confinement of the Insured due to injury or illness up to 365 days

Long-term Hospitalization (LTH) - Pays additional daily cash due to continuous confinement in excess of thirty (30) days until 335 days of confinement.


Optional Components:
- Intensice Care Unit Benefit 
- Long-term Hospitalization 
- Surgical Expense Reimbursement
