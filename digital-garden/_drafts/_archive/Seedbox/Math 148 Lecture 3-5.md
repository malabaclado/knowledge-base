# Math 148 Lecture 3-5
 **Concept of Duality**
 ![[Pasted image 20220303231327.png|400]]
 Example: P1 and T2, P3 and T3, P4 and T4 just interchange.

 This principle applied in [[Projective Geometry]] but not in Euclidean Geometry

There are finite and inifinite projective planes.

 **Structure of Finite Projective Planes**
 
 The smallest projective plane has to contain at least three points and at least three lines.

![[Pasted image 20220303232203.png|400]]


==Note that this theorem has a corresponding dual.==

Proof.
(1) implies (2)
- Let *l* and *l'* be distinct lines in the projective plane. ==These lines has a unique intersection.== Let $P = l\cap l'$
- By T4, there exists a line *l''* not concurrent with *l* and *l'*  
- By P3, there is a third point *Q* on *l''* that is different with $l\cap l''$ and $l' \cap l''$.
