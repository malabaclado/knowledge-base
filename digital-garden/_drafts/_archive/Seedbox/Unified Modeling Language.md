The **unified modeling language** (**UML**) is a general-purpose [modeling language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modeling_language "Modeling language") that is intended to provide a standard way to visualize the design of a system.

![](https://cdn-images.visual-paradigm.com/guide/what-is-uml/02-uml-diagram-types.png)

UML provides a standard notation for many types of diagrams which can be roughly divided into 3 main groups: behavior diagrams, interaction diagrams, and structure diagrams.^[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unified_Modeling_Language]
