# How to achieve your financial goals through life
- STEP 1: Set your financial goals 
- STEP 2: Build healthy financial habit
- STEP 3: Find the right investment for your goals (aligned w/ your timeframe)
	- Money market > Bond > Balanced > Equity
- Knowing your priorities in order to set aside your want is the best way to achieve fiancial freedom! 💖
- STEP 4: Monitor your progress and monitor your progress
	- Review and analyze your goals (yearly/quarterly)
		- How far are we from the goal?
		- Can you afford to increase your savings/investments?
		- Is your investment portfolio still appropriate to your situation?
- Tips
	- For students who dont earn yet:
		- Control your expenses
		- Find a way to have a source of income
	- *Time in the market is money in the market*
- Q&A
	- common mistakes
		- mistmatch between funds and goal
		- being hasty and unrealistic
	- how much emergency fund berfore investing?
		- 6-months worth of your expenses
	- tips on how not to deviate from your goals?
		- process control (remind yourself)
		- practical: isulat mo
	- given the current high inflation, how can investors achieve their financial goals?
		- as much as you can, invest in the stock market (invest for the long term)
	- recommended books/resources?
		- COL financial
		- Stocks for the long run by Jeremy Siegel
	- Is money market fund still worth it given the higher inflation?
		- use money market ONLY for conservation
		- potentially, pag mataas yung inflation mataas din ang rate sa money market
