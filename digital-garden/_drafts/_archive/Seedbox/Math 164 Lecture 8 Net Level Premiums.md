# Math 164 Lecture 8 Net Level Premiums

**Loss Random Variable**
The loss random variable represents the difference of the present value of benefits and present value of premiums at specific time t. 

![[Pasted image 20220519160757.png]]

---
**The Equivalence Principle**
The expected loss at $t=0$ is $0$. 
![[Pasted image 20220519160837.png]]

---
**Fully Continuous Net Level Premiums**
The death benefit is payable at the moment of death while premiums are payable monthly. 

![[Pasted image 20220519161013.png]]

---
**Fully Discrete Net Level Premiums** 
Death benefit is payable at the end of year of death while premiums are payable at the beginning of each year. 

![[Pasted image 20220519161121.png]]

---
**Semi-continuous Net Level Premium**
Death benefit is payable at the moment of death while premiums are payable at the beginning of each year. 

![[Pasted image 20220519161238.png]]
