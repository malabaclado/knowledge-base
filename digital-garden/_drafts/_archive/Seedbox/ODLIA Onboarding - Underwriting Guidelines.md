**Question** ::❓**Clarification:** Si PRU ang magsasabi kung need ni client ng medical thru PRUOne? As in hindi po tayo ang magdedecide kung need ni client. *Answer: Yes. Si pru ang mageevaluate kung need ba ni client ng medical exam. Libre naman po ito.*
