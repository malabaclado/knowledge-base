# Leg Raises
**Progressing to Regular Leg Raise**
Knee Tucks (Goal: 3x40)
Knee Raises (Goal: 3x40)
- Don't touch the floor
Bent Leg Raise - 45deg (Goal 3x35)
Regular Leg Raise (Goal: 2x25)

**Progressing to Hanging Leg Raise**
Hanging Knee Raise
Hanging Bent Leg Raise
Hanging Leg Raise

Isometrics (6x30 seconds)
- L-Hold
- V-Hold
- Hanging V-hold


Tip:
- Inhale on your way up, exhale on your way down
- Suck in your stomach
- Wait an hour after eating before doing leg raises
- Don't rely on momentum
- Stretch (esp. hamstring) before doing leg raises
- Follow isometrics with moving exercise
