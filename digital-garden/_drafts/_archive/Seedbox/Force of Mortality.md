# Force of Mortality
[[Force of Mortality]] refers to the instantaneous rate of change in the mortality probability of an individual. It is also known as [[Hazard Rate]]. It is often denoted by $\mu(x+t)$ or $\mu_{x+t}$.

Form 1 ^f1f8af

$$\mu_{x+t} = \frac{F'_x(t)}{1-F_{x}(t)}$$
