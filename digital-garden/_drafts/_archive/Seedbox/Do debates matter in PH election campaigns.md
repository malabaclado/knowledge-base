# Do debates matter in PH election campaigns
Aries Arugay (UP Dept of PolSci)
Antonio Contreras (DLSU Dept of PolSci)
Date: Feb 2022 

---

Debates no longer help people decide bc people already decided - contreras 

The narratives clashing rn are very powerful narratives. 

Debates rn just become additional noise - contreras 

Reactions seen from these debates does not change people's minds - partisans always be on their party. - contreras 

Debates become a material for spinning no longer for discerning - contreras 

There is selective bias - not everyone watches the debates. Let's treat debates as another source of information. - Aries

Context: Debates matter not because voters will learn from it but when candidates don't show up, we don't know what effects it will have. 

*Does failure to attend debates affect a candidates' position?* 

Its too early to say it will not have an effect - Aries 

GMA and PNOY were also selective in their attendance in their debates. NO hard data to say how selectivity affects candidacy - Contreras 

Campaigns right now are very intimate. People are no longer just voters, they are even campaign workers, they produce their own content - this is becoming a participatory form of campainging. - Contreras 

*On clashing narratives of redemption and retribution*

These narratives exemplify polarization. What we need is to depolarize. 


*Going by the narratives of redemption vs retribution, bakit parang mas kawawa pa sila marcos?*

This is what we see in the discrouse. As much as we want to not have polarization, the polarization exists and is no longer just among the elites anymore, its also among the ordinary people. - Contreras 

**The winning point of this election is to depolarize.**

**The clashing narratives create a toxic environment for voters to create a proper discourse.**

**Debates become a material for spinning - further strengthening depolarization, rather than a material for discerning the right candidate.**
