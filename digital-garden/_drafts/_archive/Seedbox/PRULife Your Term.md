# PRULife Your Term
---
![[Pasted image 20220614191005.png]]

---

**PRULife Your Term (PYT) is a yearly renewable term plan package that offers comprehensive coverage for death, accident, and disability.**

**Solution for:** Offers protection and ready-fund for critical illness, that is affordable.

**Hot buttons**
- If you are between 25 to 34 years old with an average monthly income of Php 18,000, you would want to have a headstart in your income protection portfolio.
- As a breadwinner, I understand you have a lot of responsibilities to fulfill and I am sure you want to secure your family’s future in case something unexpected happens to you.
- If part of your medium- to long-term goal is to buy a house or condominium, then you will need a Mortgage Redemption Insurance (MRI) for your bank loan.
- If you wish to conserve your estate and leave it as a legacy to your heir/s, life insurance protected against estate tax (calculated with a computation tool) is an excellent option.
- [[Supporting Facts]]

**Notes**
- It's high protection coverage makes it a good product for [[Mortgage Redemption Insurance]] (MRI) and estate conservation purposes.
- [[PRULife Your Term]] is only available in Philippine pesos.
- Remind clients to pay premiums within the grace period to avoid policy termination.

**Key Features:**
- High coverage at minimal cost.
- Renewable (yearly without need of medical)
- Convertible to investment-linked *from the beginning of the second year.* 
- Can attach [[Variable Life Rider]]

**Details**
- Issuable age: 18 to 60 (SA depends on age)
- Duration: One year (option to renew until age 89)
- Payment: Once per renewal
- Premium:  Initial premium is determined on the basis of the benefits and amounts applied for as well as the Life Insured’s age upon enrollment.
- Cooling off period: 15 days
- The policyowner and life insured must be the same person for the online application to be acceptable.
- Can add riders - PA, Hospital, CI (also Variable Life Rider attachable)
- No investment, but can attach [[Variable Life Rider]]

**Benefits and Limitation**
- [[Two-year contestability period]] 
- [[Juvenile Lien]] is DOES NOT apply.
- Full underwriting required if exceeded agents' [[Non-Medical Authority]], [[Riders]] are attached and if the issue age is 70 years old and above.

**Attachable Riders**
- Disability: ATPD 
- Critical Illness: ALCB 
- Accident: ADD, PA 
- Hospitalization: DHI 
- Waiver: WPTPD, LCW, PW
- Investment: VL

## Benefits
- Death benefit = sum assured
- ATPD = 50% sum assured
- ADD = 100% sum assured
- TPD Waiver

## Regulation and Compliance 
- Client must be informed during product presentation that the Policy has no Cash Value and does not share in the Company’s surplus earnings, also known as dividends.
-  Client must be informed that if no premium payment for the next renewal payment is made, the policy will terminate.
-  Client must be informed that any time after the first policy year, PRULife Your Term may be converted to a whole life or variable investment-linked policy, subject to terms and conditions.
- Clients must be provided with accurate and complete information including appropriate disclosures with respect to exclusions of any pre-existing conditions, the 15-day cooling off period and the withdrawal conditions which are both applicable for the Variable Life Rider only.
