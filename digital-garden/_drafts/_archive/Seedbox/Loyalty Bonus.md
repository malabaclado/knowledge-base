# Loyalty Bonus
The basic qualification to be eligible for loyalty bonus is continuous payment of premiums for the first 10 years. No missed premiums and no withdrawals. 

If your client paid his premiums from year 1 to year 10, this will entitle him to receive 10% of his annual premium starting on the 11th year up to the 20th year for PAA Plus plans and 11th year up to 15th year for Elite 15 plans  as long as he continues paying for his premiums.

Loyalty bonuses are given at the start of the year.
