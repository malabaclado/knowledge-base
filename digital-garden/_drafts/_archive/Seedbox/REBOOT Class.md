## The 4 Arts of a Prudential Agent

1. **[[Matching solutions to your clients' needs|MATCHING]].** You are able to match the need of a client to a solution. 
2. **[[Deepening the relationship with your prospects|DEEPENING]].** Deepening your relationship with your prospects. 
3. **[[How you represent yourself as an insurance agent|REPRESENTING]].** How do you carry yourself? How do you carry the conversation? How do you build your image?
4. **TOOLING.**  How efficiently do you use the tools?

## From small talk to business talk
Segway topics to business talk. Choose one of these elements and ask questions to your prospect. Deepen the relationship by asking follow-up questions. 
1. Occupation 
2. Health status 
3. Lifestyle & Hobbies 
4. Date of birth/age 
5. Dependents 
6. Financial Dreams and Goals 
7. General Dreams and Goals 
8. Existing Coverage

> A client is like a door. You cannot close a door if the door is already closed. You can only close a client if you've opened their minds to the possibilities. 

 Example:
 - Occupation: *"So you're working -- hours a day, earning -- in a month. What do you think would happen if ever you get sick or for some reason, you cannot go to work anymore? What if I show you something that could protect your income in case of these emergencies?"*
