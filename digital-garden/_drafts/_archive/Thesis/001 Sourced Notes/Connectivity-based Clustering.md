
**Connectivity-based clustering** models are based on the notion that the data points closer in data space exhibit more similarity to each other than the data points lying farther away.

# Two Approaches
There are two approaches for connectivity-based clustering algorithms:
1. Classify all data points into separate clusters -> Aggregate these points as distance decreases.
2. Classify all data points as a single cluster -> Partition the cluster as the distance increases.


The choice of distance function is subjective.

# Pros and Cons

PROS
- easy to interpret

CONS
- lacks scalability for handling big datasets
