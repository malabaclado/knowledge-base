Spark is a framework for working with big data.

The goal of Spark is to provide a fast, general-purpose cluster framework for large-scale processing. The foundation of Spark is based on the *resilient distributed dataset (RDD)*.  

Common  libraries spark libraries include: Spark SQL, Spark Streaming, MLLib, GraphX  

The most important feature of spark is speed.
