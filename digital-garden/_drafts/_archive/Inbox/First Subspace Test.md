# Theorem: First Subspace Test
A non-empty subset $W$ of a vector space $V$ is a subspace of $V$ if and only if it is closed under addition and scalar mulltiplication; that is, 
- $w_{1},w_{2} \in W$ implies $w_{1}+w_{2} \in W$ and 
- $c\in \mathbb{F}, w\in W$ implies $cw \in W$

# Corrolary: Second Subspace Test
A non-empty subset $W$ of the vector space $V$ over $\mathbb{F}$ is a subspace if and only if, for any $c_{1}, c_{2} \in \mathbb{F}$ and for any $w_{1}, w_{2} \in W$m, we have $c_{1}w_{1} + c_{2}w_{2} \in W$
