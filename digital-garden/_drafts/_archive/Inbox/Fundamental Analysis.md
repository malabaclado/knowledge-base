# Fundamental Analysis

Fundamental analysis is a method to estimate intrinsic value of a stock by analyzing various internal and external factors that could have effects on the value of stock or company. 

The fundamental factors include business environment, financial performance, economic data, and social and political behaviour. [[@obthongSurveyMachineLearning2020]]
