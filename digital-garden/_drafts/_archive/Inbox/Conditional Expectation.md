# Conditional Expectation
The conditional expectation is given by:
$$\mathbb{E}_{x}[f|y] = \sum_{x}p(x|y) f(x)$$
where $\mathbb{E}_{x}[f|y]$ denote the expectation of the conditional distribution with respect to $x$.
