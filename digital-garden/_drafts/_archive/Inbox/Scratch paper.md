The total surface area of the right square pyramid is given by:
$$SA = (4 \times (\text{area of triangle}) )+ \text{area of square} $$
Given that the area of a triangle is $\frac{1}{2}bh$, the area of the square is $b^2$ where base $b=8$, we have:
$$SA = \left( 4 \times \frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times h\right) + 8^2$$
To find h, we rearrange the equation:
$$h = \frac{SA - 8^2}{16}$$

Solving for height $h$, we get:
$$h=5$$
Solving for the volume of the square based pyramid:
$$V = \frac{1}{3}b^{2}h = \frac{1}{3}(8^{2})(5) = 106.67$$
