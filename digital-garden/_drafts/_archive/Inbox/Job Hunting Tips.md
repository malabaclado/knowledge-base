All job interviews ask three _and only three_ questions. If you get asked something that isn't one of them, _answer as if it is._ If you think the interviewer forgot to ask one of them, _make sure you still answer it during the interview._

- Can you do the job?
- Do you want the job?
- Can you fit in?

---
When asked "Tell me about yourself", on an interview, you can prep an answer by choosing 2-3 key traits about yourself that fit the role.
