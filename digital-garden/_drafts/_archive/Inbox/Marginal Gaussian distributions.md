# Marginal Gaussian distributions
We have seen that if a joint distribution $p(\text{x}_{a}, \text{x}_{b})$ is Gaussian, then the [[Conditional Gaussian|conditional distribution]] is also Gaussian . 

The marginal distribution given by:$$p(\text{x}_{a}) = \int p(\text{x}_{a} , \text{x}_{b} )d\text{x}_b$$
is also Gaussian.
