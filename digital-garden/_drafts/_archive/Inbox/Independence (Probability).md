Let $X$ and $Y$ be random variables. We say $X$ and $Y$ are independent random variables if and only if: $$P(X=x, Y=y) = P(X=x) \cdot P(Y=y)$$
for all $x \in S_{1}$ and $y\in S_{2}$  where $S_{1},S_{2}$ are the supports of $X$ and $Y$ respectively.
