Topic: Transportation and Allowance of Students in Dasmarinas City, Cavite

Respondents:
- Must be a high school/senior high school/undergraduate/graduate student living in Dasmarinas City.
- Must now own or use a privately-owned vehicle on their way to school.
- Must use at least one public transportation such as tricycle, jeepney, bus, transit, ride-sharing service (such as Grab/Angkas).
- 


Survey Questions
[Q1] How old are you? 
[Q2] What is your gender?
[Q3] What is your education level? (Multiple Choice: High School Level, Senior High School Level, Undergraduate Level, Graduate Level)
[Q4] How much is your total weekly allowance? (in Philippine Pesos)
[Q5] How much is your daily expense for transportation? (in Philippine Pesos)
[Q6] On average, how many kilometers do you travel each day on your commute to school? (in kilometers)
[Q7] On average, what is the usual waiting time for you to secure transportation? (in minutes)
[Q8] How long does it typically take you to reach your school or educational institution from your residence in Dasmarinas City? (in minutes)
[Q9] How often do you use tricycles for your daily commute to school? (Multiple choice: Daily, 2-3 times a week, Once a week, 2-3 times a month, Once a month, I don't use it)
[Q10] How often do you use jeepneys for your daily commute to school? (Multiple choice: Daily, 2-3 times a week, Once a week, 2-3 times a month, Once a month, I don't use it)
[Q11] How often do you use buses for your daily commute to school? (Multiple choice: Daily, 2-3 times a week, Once a week, 2-3 times a month, Once a month, I don't use it)
[Q12] How often do you use transit for your daily commute to school? (Multiple choice: Daily, 2-3 times a week, Once a week, 2-3 times a month, Once a month, I don't use it)
[Q13] How often do you use ride-sharing services (such as Grab/Angkas) for your daily commute to school? (Multiple choice: Daily, 2-3 times a week, Once a week, 2-3 times a month, Once a month, I don't use it)
[Q14] On a rainy day, which public transportation are you most likely to use? (Tricycle, Jeepney, Bus, Transit, Grab/Angkas)
[Q15] On a rainy day, which public transportation are you most likely to avoid? (Tricycle, Jeepney, Bus, Transit, Grab/Angkas)
