# Strategies to improve business
## Enhance local visibility
- Regularly post content on FB page
	- Post educational content
	- Post trending content
	- Post on holidays content
- Increase presence on Paliparan 3 Facebook Group
## Special offers
- Limited-time promotions like battery replacements, free services for referrals.
- Product packages (eg. watch + necklace for 1500 discounted price)
- **Bundle Services:** Create service packages that combine watch repairs with other offerings, such as battery replacements, strap adjustments, or cleaning. Bundles can encourage customers to avail multiple services.
- **Loyalty Cards:** Introduce loyalty cards that offer a free service after a certain number of visits. This encourages repeat business and establishes a loyal customer base.
## Complementary products
- Watch chains/cuffs
- Vintage/Modern clocks
- Jewelry: Bracelets, necklaces, earrings
- Gift items: Keychains, cufflinks, tie clips
- Digital Watches
## Branding aesthetic
1. **Classic Elegance:** A timeless and sophisticated aesthetic with rich wood furnishings, soft lighting, and luxurious details. This creates an atmosphere of refined craftsmanship that resonates with traditional watch enthusiasts.
2. **Modern Minimalism:** Clean lines, open spaces, and a minimalist color palette create a contemporary and sleek ambiance. This aesthetic can highlight the beauty of the watches themselves and provide a modern backdrop.
3. **Industrial Chic:** Incorporate raw materials like exposed brick, metal accents, and industrial lighting. This aesthetic balances ruggedness with sophistication, appealing to a diverse range of customers.
4. **Vintage Nostalgia:** Decorate with vintage clocks, antique furniture, and warm, aged color tones. This nostalgic aesthetic can evoke a sense of history and charm that resonates with watch enthusiasts.
5. **Tech-Inspired:** Employ sleek and futuristic design elements that align with the precision and innovation of watches. Use metallic finishes, neon lighting, and digital displays for a high-tech look.
6. **Nature-Inspired:** Create a calming environment with natural elements like wood, plants, and soft lighting. This aesthetic can promote relaxation and reflection, aligning with the intricacy of watch craftsmanship.
7. **Luxury Boutique:** Incorporate plush furnishings, ornate details, and opulent materials like velvet and marble. This upscale aesthetic creates an exclusive and indulgent shopping experience.
8. **Geometric Modernism:** Utilize bold geometric shapes, abstract patterns, and unique fixtures to create a visually striking and artistic atmosphere.
9. **Cultural Fusion:** Blend elements from different cultures or regions, incorporating diverse textures, colors, and decorative items to create a captivating and globally inspired environment.
10. **Interactive Innovation:** Incorporate interactive displays, touchscreens, and digital technology that engage customers in a dynamic and immersive shopping experience.
11. **Monochrome Chic:** Embrace a single color palette, such as black and white, for a high-contrast and visually impactful aesthetic.
12. **Heritage and Storytelling:** Showcase the history and stories behind watches through displays, framed narratives, and vintage memorabilia that immerse customers in the world of horology.
