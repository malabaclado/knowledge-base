When the levels of a factor are selected randomly from a long list of options for the levels, the factor is called the random factor. The analysis of variance model used for a random factor is called the **random effects model**.

In a random effect model, you test on variance rather than the mean.

Example Hypothesis:
- H0: Variances are equal
- HA: Any pair of varaince is not equal.
