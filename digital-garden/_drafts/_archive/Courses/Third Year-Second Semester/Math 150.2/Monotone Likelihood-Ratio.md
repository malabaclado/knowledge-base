> **Definition. (Monotone Likelihood-Ratio)**
> A family of densities $\{f(x;\theta) | \theta\in \Omega \}$ is said to have a **monotone likelihood ratio (MLR)** if there exists a statistics $T=t(X_1,X_2,...,X_n)$ such that the ratio $$\frac{L(\theta'; x_1,x_2,...,x_n)}{L(\theta''; x_1,x_2,...,x_n)}$$ is either a:
> - **non-increasing function** of $t(x_1,x_2,...,x_n)$ for every $\theta' < \theta''$; or
> - **non-decreasing function** $t(x_1,x_2,...,x_n)$ for every $\theta' < \theta''$
