> Definition. (Irreducible Polynomial for $\alpha$ over $F$)
> The unique [[monic]] polynomial $p(x)$ in the previous theorem is called the** irreducible polynomial for $\alpha$ over F (or the minimal polynomial for α over F) and is denoted by $irr(\alpha, F)$. The degree of $p(x) = irr(\alpha, F)$ is called the **degree of $\alpha$ over F** and is denoted by $deg(\alpha, F)$.
