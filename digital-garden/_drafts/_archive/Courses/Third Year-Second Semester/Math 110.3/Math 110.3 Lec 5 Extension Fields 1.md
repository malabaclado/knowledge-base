### Notes
In this chapter, we discuss the definition of an [[Extension Field]].

Next, we discuss a theorem about the *existence of extension fields*. This theorem also says that even if we cannot find a zero for a polynomial in F[x], we can *"extend"* the field so as to arrive at a zero for that polynomial. It is known as [[Kronecker's Theorem]]
