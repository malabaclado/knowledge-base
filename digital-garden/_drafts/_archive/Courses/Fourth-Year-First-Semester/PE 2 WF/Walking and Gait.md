
**What is Gait?**
- It is the manner of walking
- The act of moving of one foot over the other or  foot sequences.

### Types of Walks
1. Normal Walk
	- Simplest form. A contra-lateral arm swing accompanies it.


2. Stroll
	- Slower than normal walk. May or may not have an accompanied arm swing.

3. Brisk Walk
- Faster than normal walk. This is the usual pace of the world.

4. Power Walk
	- "Exercise walk"
	- Has faster pace than normal walk. Borderline jogging.
	- Arms are a little bit exaggerated.

5. Strut
	- Has certain pause for every step
	- Common in pageants
	- Steps follow a straight line

6. Tiptoe
	- Gait has minimal contact of the foot with the ground.

7. March
	- Gait is in high knees
	- Has a rhythm
	- Exaggerated arm swing

8. Hobble
	- Just small steps.
	- Foot movement is quicker than usual.
	- Usually indicates a problem or injury.

9. Limp
	- Similar to hobble.
	- Has a dragging movement.

10. Competitive
	- Requires a lot of technique
	- More hip movement
	- Arm swing is important
