
1. Colegio de San Juan de Letran (1630)
- Its purpose was to educate and mold orphans into good Christian citizens.

- It was a merge of two institutions:
	1. Colegio de Niños Huerfanos de San Juan de Letran
		- Founded in 1620 by Don Juan Geronimo Guerrero, a retired Spanish Officer
		- Its purpose was to educate and mold orphans into good Christian citizens.
	2. Colegio de Huerfanos de San Pedro y San Pablo
		- Established by the Dominican brother Diego de Santa Maria at the Convent of Santo Domingo.

2. Colegio de Nuestra Señora del Santísimo Rosario (1611)
- Founded on April 28, 1611

- In 1619, renamed _Colegio de Santo Tomas_
- Elevated to a universoty in 1645
- Now known as University of Santo Tomas
