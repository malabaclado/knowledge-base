
Proposition. 
Let $X$ be a topological space and $Y\subset X$. If $Y$ is open in $X$ and $U$ is open in $Y$, then $U$ is open on $X$.

Proof.
Suppose $U$ is open in $Y$. It is implied that $U = Y\cap U'$ for some open set $U'$ in $X$. Since $Y$ is open in X and $U'$ is open in X, it follows that $U = Y\cap U'$ is open in $X$.
