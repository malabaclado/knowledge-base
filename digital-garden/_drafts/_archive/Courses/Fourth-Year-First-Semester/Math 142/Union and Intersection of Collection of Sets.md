
The **union of a collection** of sets $\mathcal{S}$ contains all elements $x$ that *can be found in any set* $U \in \mathcal{S}$ in the collection.

The **intersection of a collection** of sets $\mathcal{S}$ contains all elements $x$ that *can be found in every set* $U \in \mathcal{S}$ in the collection.

### Formal Definition

> **Definition.** Let $\mathcal{S}$ be a collection of sets.
> - The union of all sets in the collection is defined as $$\bigcup_{U \in \mathcal{S}} U = \{x| (\exists U \in \mathcal{S})(x \in U)\}$$
> - The intersection of all sets in the collection is defined as $$\bigcap_{U \in \mathcal{S}} U = \{x| (\forall U \in \mathcal{S})(x \in U)\}$$
