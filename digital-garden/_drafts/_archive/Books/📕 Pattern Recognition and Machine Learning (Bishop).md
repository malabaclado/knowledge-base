### 1.5 Decision Theory
- _Probability theory_ provides the framework for quantifying and manipulating uncertainty. *Decision Theory* helps us establish a criteria on how to classify things.
- Suppose we want to predict a target variable $\textbf{t}$ given a new input variable $\textbf{x}$. Our goal is **to minimize the chance of assigning $\textbf{x}$ to the wrong class** (ie. diagnosing a cancer even though the parient has no cancer). To do this, we must *choose the class having the highest posterior probability*.
