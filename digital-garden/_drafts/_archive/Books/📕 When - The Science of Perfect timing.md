*We think timing is an art, but it really is a science.*

Three ways to turn a slump into a spark:
1. Be aware of these midpoints
2. Use them as a wakeup call
3. Imagine you're behind so you can propel yourself into action

*At the beginnning of a pursuit, we're generally more motivated by how far we progressed. At the end, we're generally more energized by trying to close the small gap that remains.*

*People are more likely to be persuaded to overcome the default "No" early in the day, or immediately after breaks.*

- Q: When should you begin a new diet/exercise program?
- A: Temporal landmark - there are certain dates when people are more likely to embark on behavior change.
	- Example: New year, Start of a new semester, Start of a new month

*Our lives are in some ways, episodic.*

*One of the things endings do is endings help us energize. When the end of something becomes salient, we kick a little harder.*

*When we synchronize with other people, we feel better - and more likely to cooperate and collaborate.*

The Synchrony Effect
- You want to line up the chronotype, task and time.
- Figure out what you want to do when at you're best. Then give yourself those guidelines when you're at you're worst. 

Peak - High mood, High vigilance
Trough - Low mood, Medium vigilance
Recovery - High mood, low vigilance

Peak - Planning, Decision Making
Trough - Mechanical work, Routine work
Recovery - Socializing, Meetings

Vigilance is your level of self-control to battle distractions.

Two kinds of breaks: restorative breaks / vigilance breaks

Restorative breaks
- Take more breaks
- Moving breaks rather than stationary
- Full detachment
- Outside rather than inside

Vigilance breaks
- for high stakes encounters
- taking a step back, go through a list of what you need to do

Q: When to have caffeine? 
A: Takes 25 minutes to hit. Don´t have it immediately when you wake up. (Best: after 1-2 hrs, when your cortisol levels begin to drop).

A: 10-20 minutes. Ex: sit on your chair at office, drink a quick coffee/caffeine,  put on noise-cancelling, timer on 25 minutes (in case it takes 5-10 minutes to fall asleep).
