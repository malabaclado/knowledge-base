- Tips for developing your career objective as a recent graduate — [Updated on 2023-03-15 14:23:25](https://hyp.is/5HDuiML5Ee2YBKsoU2FzsQ/www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/objective-in-resume-for-fresh-graduate) — Group: #Just-Me
    - Tags:  ##resumewriting 
    - Annotation: 1. Keep it short. Hiring managers and recruiters don't have a lot of time to look through every part of your resume, so keep your resume objective short, impactful and easy to read.

2. Highlight your best attributes. Because you don't have a lot of space with which to explain why you're a good fit for the position you're applying for, make sure to highlight the skills you have that are most relevant to the position.

3. Adjust for each application. Each time you apply for a position, model after what you see in the job description. You should have a sense of what a company is looking for, or what skills are most important for the new employee to have, and you can be sure to include words and phrases that fit well.

4. Have someone review your objective. It's a good idea to have someone else review your resume, including your objective, to make sure it's clear, enticing and markets you as a potential new hire.

5. Use paragraph form. While you may use bullet points in other parts of your resume, use paragraph form when you write your resume objective.
