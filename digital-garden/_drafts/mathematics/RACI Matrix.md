==A **RACI matrix**, also known as a RACI chart or RACI model, is a **tool used in project management** and organizational design to clarify and define the roles and responsibilities of individuals or teams within a specific project or process.== "RACI" stands for Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed, which are the key roles or levels of involvement for individuals or groups in a project or process:

1. **Responsible (R)**: The person or group who is responsible for completing a specific task or activity. They are the "doers" and are in charge of getting the work done.
	1. **Accountable (A)**: The person who is ultimately accountable for the success of the project or process. This person makes the final decisions and ensures that the work is completed.  There should be only one "A" for each task or activity.
2. **Consulted (C)**: Individuals or groups who provide input, expertise, or feedback on the task or activity. They are not responsible for doing the work, but their input is valuable to its successful completion.
3. **Informed (I)**: People or teams who need to be kept in the loop about the progress of a task or activity. They receive updates and information but are not directly involved in the work.

The RACI matrix is typically presented as a table, with tasks or activities listed on one axis and the individuals or groups involved on the other axis. Each task/activity is assigned a single letter (R, A, C, or I) for each person or group involved to clarify their role in that specific task or activity.

The RACI matrix helps prevent confusion, overlaps, or gaps in responsibilities, ensuring that everyone knows what is expected of them and who to turn to for questions or decisions. It is a valuable tool for improving communication, accountability, and efficiency in projects and processes.
