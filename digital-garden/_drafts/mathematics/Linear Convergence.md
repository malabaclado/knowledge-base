==**Definition.**== Let $e_i$ denote the error at step $i$ of an iterative method. The method is said to obey **linear convergence** with rate S if $$S=\lim_{i\to \infty}\frac{e_{i+1}}{e_i}$$

Linearly convergent if the index/exponent of the denominator is 1.
