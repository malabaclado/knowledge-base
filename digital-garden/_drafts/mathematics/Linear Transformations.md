# Linear Transformations
A linear transformation is a function $f: V \to W$ that satisfies the following conditions:
1. For any two vectors,  $v_{1},v_{2} \in V$, the following equation holds: 
$$f(v_{1}+ v_{2}) = f(v_{1}) + f(v_{2})$$ 
1. For any scalar $r \in \mathbb{F}$ and any vector $u \in V$, the following equation holds: $$f(r \cdot u) = r \cdot f(u)$$
