Let $f$ be a linear transformation. The kernel of $f$ is defined as the set: $$\text{ker}(f) = f^{-1}(\{0_{W}\})=\{v \in V| f(v) = 0_{W} \}$$

The kernel of $f$ is the set of all elements of $V$ which are mapped to the zero vector in $W$. 

Note that the kernel of a linear transformation is a vector space itself. The dimension of $\text{ker}(f)$ is called the [[nullity]] of $f$.
