[[Homework 3 Transcript -  Extension Fields]]

---
Our basic goal is to show that every nonconstant polynomial has a zero. We do this by creating an extension field.

> ==Definition.== Extension Field
> A field $E$ is an extension field of field $F$ if $F \leq E$

Example: $\mathbb{R}$ is an extension of $\mathbb{Q}$. $\mathbb{C}$ is an extension of both $\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathbb{Q}$.

---

> ==Theorem.== [[Kronecker's Theorem]]
> Let $F$ be a field and let $f(x)$ be a nonconstant polynomial in $F[x]$. Then there exists an extension field $E$ of $F$ and an $\alpha \in E$ such that $f (\alpha) = 0$.


> ==Creating an Extension Field via Kronecker's Theorem==
> 1. Take a polynomial $f(x) \in F[x]$
> 2. Take an irreducible factor $p(x)$ of $f(x)$. 
> 3. Take maximal ideal $\langle p(x)\rangle$ generated by $p(x)$. 
> 4. Create the quotient ring $F/\langle p(x)\rangle$. This is a field and is an extension of the field $F$.

Remark: 
- In step 2: We are sure of the existence of such factor because $F$ is a Unique Factorization Domain.
- In step 3: Recall that an irreducible polynomialp(x) over a field $F$ gives rise to a maximal ideal $\langle p(x)\rangle$ of the polynomial ring $F[x]$.
