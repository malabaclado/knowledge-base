*What are the benefits of connecting with other people? Why you should want to have genuine connections with other people?*
1. **Connect and live longer.** People who stay socially and physicaly active live longer.
2. **Connect and get cooperation.** If people like you, there would be high chances of them giving you their time and effort.
3. **Connect and feel safe.** We all have a basic need for other people - shared, mutual benefits in a community and the feeling of "looking out for each other".
4. **Connect and feel love.** People benefit from being supported and validated.
