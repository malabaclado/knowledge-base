*What are the benefits of being likable?*
- If people like you, they feel natural and comfortable around you. They will give you their attention and happily open up for you.
- Likability has something to do about how you look but a lot more to do with how you make people feel. 
- Likable people give loud and clear signals of their willingness to be sociable -- they reveal that their public communication channels are open.
