# Maximum Likelihood for AG
**Finite Mixture of Asymmetric Gaussian**
We say that a D-dimensional random variable $X=\{X_1,X_2,...,X_D\}$ follows a K-components mixture if its probability function can be written in the following form: $$p(X)=\sum_{k=1}^{K}\pi_{k}p(X|\theta^{(k)})$$ where $\theta^{(k)}$ is the set of parameters of component $k$ and  $\pi_{i}$ are the mixing coefficients .
