The daily end goal is to have clear Today list and Habit lists.

End-of-day Routine
1. Manage tasks (Process today and inbox)
2. Next-day planning
3. End-of-day wrap-up reflection


Manage tasks
1. Process and empty inbox
2. Handling overdue tasks -- either schedule them tomorrow or clear date and tag them `#overflow`.
	- The reason for this is to keep the consistency within my plans and to avoid over-rescheduling tiny details.
	- Overflow tasks could be done on free time or on Saturdays -- where I can sacrifice some leisure time.
3. Clear today list


How to do Next-Day Planning:
1. Check overflow. If there are tasks here, prioritize them OR reschedule them.
2. Check eisenhower matrix. If there are Quadrant I tasks, do Quadrant II tasks.
3. Check specific projects if there are things that needs doing.
