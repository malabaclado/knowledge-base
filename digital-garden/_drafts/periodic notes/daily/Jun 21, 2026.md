- FIRE Movement
	- At its core, **FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early)** is a mathematical formula wrapped in a lifestyle philosophy. The central objective is to accumulate a pool of income-producing assets large enough that you never _have_ to work for money again.
- House Turnover Checklist
	- What to bring
		- Camera - *for documentation*
		- Water - *for leak test*
		-  Measuring tape
		- Levelling tool
		- Electric tester
	- What to check
		- Entryways
			- Take time to have a good look at the quality of workmanship of exterior finishes. Make sure that all surfaces are correctly and evenly applied. At ground level, make sure that no cracks are present in the driveway, garage floor and patio.
			- Check lock holes on doors and windows
		- Kitchen
			- Make sure that all countertops and the front of each cabinet have smooth finishes, free from blemishes and scratches. 
			- Try opening each and every cabinet and drawer to see if everything can be easily pulled in and out.
		- Bathroom
		- Utilities
			- Water utility
			- Electric utility
		- In terms of electrical work, always start with the main circuit breaker. Make sure that it is located somewhere accessible to all members of the family. Check if all labels coincide with the switches, and consequently, make sure that these switches do work.
		- Sockets 
			- Every room should have 2 sockets
			- Sockets should be working
		- Sink
			- Check leaks in pipes
