If you don’t have an obsessive career calling right now, stop hunting for one. Instead, treat your career as a craftsman treats their trade: an engine that builds rare, valuable skills. Focus on becoming exceptionally reliable and technically sharp at what is in front of you. When you accumulate real competence, you gain leverage—and leverage is what eventually buys you autonomy, flexible hours, good pay, and the breathing room to travel and enjoy life on your own terms.

**Purpose lives in your personal standard of execution**
- Showing up on your workouts
- Following up on your set goals
- Being sincere
- Being dependable to the people who rely on you

***Action generates clarity; thinking about action only generates fog.***

**Separate "Purpose" from "Work"** 
Your career is an economic exchange; your life is where purpose resides. Caring for your family, being a steady friend, exploring a new city, getting lost in an immersive story or game, and keeping your mind and body strong are not "distractions" from your real life—they _are_ the life. If your career enables you to live with low stress, financial independence, and the capacity to explore the world, it is already doing its job brilliantly.
