**Attachment styles are ==used to describe patterns of attachment in romantic relationships==.** The concept of attachment styles grew out the attachment theory and research that emerged throughout the 1960s and 1970s.

**==Attachment styles form during early childhood== and are ==centered on how children and parents interact==**. Today, psychologists typically recognize four main attachment styles.

1. [[Secure Attachment]]
2. [[Ambivalent Attachment]]
3. [[Avoidant Attachment]]
4. [[Disorganized Attachment]]
