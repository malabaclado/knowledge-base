# Chapter 1: Financial Time Series and Their Characteristics

Most financial studies involve returns, instead of prices of assets. Here's two reasons why:
1. For the average investor, the return of an asset provides a **complete (entire outcome, whether gain or loss) and scale-free (regardless of price and currency) summary** of the investment opportunity, making different assets directly comparable.
2. Asset prices are typically *nonstationary* (having no fixed level). In contrast, price returns are much easier to model because they exhibit **more attractive statistical properties**, such as *weak stationarity* (where the mean and covariances are constant over time).
