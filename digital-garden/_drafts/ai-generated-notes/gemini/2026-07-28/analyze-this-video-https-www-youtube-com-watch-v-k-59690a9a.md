> In his video **"I'm 30. If you’re in your 20s, watch this,"** creator Daniel Braun reflects on turning 30 and shares 20 life and financial lessons he learned over the past decade \[[00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=0)\]:
> 
> 1.  **You won't get your career right on the first try** \[[00:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=8)\] – Most people make career decisions between ages 18–20 with minimal real-world experience. Expect to switch roles or industries until you find what fits.
>     
> 2.  **Take asymmetric risks** \[[00:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=44)\] – Pursue opportunities where the potential upside massively outweighs the worst-case downside, especially in your 20s when financial liabilities (like mortgages or kids) are low.
>     
> 3.  **Budgeting brings financial peace of mind** \[[01:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=96)\] – Tracking income and expenses provides clarity on your "burn rate," giving you confidence to make major life or career moves without fear.
>     
> 4.  **Travel while you are young** \[[02:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=160)\] – Don't wait until retirement to explore the world. Traveling young yields "memory dividends" that pay out for the rest of your life.
>     
> 5.  **Don't fear credit cards—learn to use them** \[[03:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=221)\] – Rather than avoiding credit cards due to fear, learn the rules, pay balances in full every month, and use rewards/points to save on travel and build credit.
>     
> 6.  **Buy every book recommended to you** \[[04:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=281)\] – A $10–$20 book condenses decades of an author's experience into a few hours of reading, yielding disproportionately massive returns on human capital.
>     
> 7.  **Invest heavily in health and fitness** \[[05:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=315)\] – Prioritize physical fitness early through gym memberships or home equipment, as higher energy levels directly translate into success across work and personal life.
>     
> 8.  **Save specifically for wedding expenses** \[[06:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=368)\] – Attending friends' and family's weddings (gifts, travel, attire) becomes a major hidden cost in your mid-to-late 20s. Set money aside in advance.
>     
> 9.  **Marry the right person** \[[06:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=416)\] – Choosing a partner with shared values, financial alignment, and ambition makes building wealth and happiness vastly easier.
>     
> 10.  **Choose your "hard"** \[[07:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=462)\] – Every choice carries difficulty (e.g., being out of shape is hard; staying fit is hard). Deliberately pick the hardships that lead to growth.
>      
> 11.  **Master delayed gratification** \[[08:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=504)\] – Resist cheap, instant dopamine traps (junk food, doom-scrolling) in favor of long-term investments in your mind, body, and career.
>      
> 12.  **Avoid sports betting** \[[09:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=556)\] – The odds are mathematically stacked in favor of the sportsbooks. Don't let normalized gambling drain your hard-earned money.
>      
> 13.  **Comparison is the thief of joy** \[[10:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=613)\] – Social media highlights only curated wins. Focus on competing with your past self rather than comparing your chapter 1 to someone else's chapter 10.
>      
> 14.  **You are the average of the five people around you** \[[11:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=664)\] – Surround yourself with ambitious, principled people, whether by moving to driven communities or actively networking online and at events.
>      
> 15.  **There is no "perfect time" for anything** \[[11:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=715)\] – Waiting for ideal timing leads to procrastination and missed opportunities. Taking imperfect action today builds momentum.
>      
> 16.  **Spend extravagantly on what you love, cut mercilessly on what you don't** \[[12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=761)\] – Avoid extreme financial scarcity mindsets; instead, spend intentionally on high-value items for yourself while eliminating fluff.
>      
> 17.  **Create friction to boost productivity and save money** \[[13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=838)\] – Remove impulse triggers (e.g., delete shopping apps, remove saved credit cards, use app blockers) so mindless spending or scrolling requires deliberate effort.
>      
> 18.  **Be a producer rather than just a consumer** \[[15:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=914)\] – Shift your mindset toward actively creating value for others, which builds income and skills, rather than spending all day consuming content.
>      
> 19.  **Get comfortable being uncomfortable** \[[15:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=955)\] – Real growth only happens when you push yourself past self-imposed boundaries and embrace unfamiliar challenges.
>      
> 20.  **It is completely okay to rent instead of buy** \[[16:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktDqOjjbsCg&t=996)\] – Homeownership isn't an immediate requirement. Renting offers flexibility, predictable costs, and cash flow preservation while you build your foundation.
