The foundation of all technical analysis is **price**. 

# Candlestick Charts
![](https://i.imgur.com/SNkfg2A.png)

What a single candlestick represent: **price movement over a period of time.** A candlestick has a body and wick, which depends on open, high, low and close (OHLC) prices.

> [!NOTE]
> - Green candle - price went lower. 
> - Red candle - price increased.


# Trending Markets 
![](https://i.imgur.com/kmnssW4.png)

Trending markets have a are great because they have a trend. *It is a good thing to trade with a trend.*

After an **impulsive move**, the market usually takes a breather. It goes down. This is called a **pullback**.

> [!NOTE]
> Impulsive moves - are moves that break a new high (in price). 

A market is still considered in an uptrend until the lowest low of the pullback is broken. ![](https://i.imgur.com/k4wxcaC.png)

# Support and Resistance
Support and resistance are simply **areas in the market** that price are likely to react from. 

![](https://i.imgur.com/hteZwRv.png)

Support are places in the market that the price is likely to go up. 


![](https://i.imgur.com/gVZQX08.png)

Resistance is an area in the market that the price is likely to go down from.

> [!NOTE]
> I think of support and resistances as ceilings and floors that can limit a maximum or a minimum price.

Three uses of support and resistances to make better trading decisions:
1. Used to spot possible reversals
2. Used to find entry points on trending markets
3. Used to determine your stops and targets
