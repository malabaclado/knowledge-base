# PRUWise MARKET PULSE - AUGUST 2022

Headline: Philippine economy grows at a slower rate amid continued increase of prices.

- Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) keeps on tighening money supply to control inflation.
- Philippine gross domestic product (GDP) grew by 7.4% year-on-year (YoY), slower than the revised Q1 2022 economic growth of 8.2%.  **This was attributed to weak agricultural production and high inflation.**  Nevertheless, in the first half, the Philippines’ economic growth averaged 7.8%, above the downscaled full-year goal of 6.5% - 7.5% and **placed as the 2nd best-performing economy in Southeast Asia after  Vietnam**. Further, the Marcos Jr. administration targets annual economic growth of 6.5% - 8.0% annually from 2023 to 2028.
- **July inflation further accelerated to 6.4% from 6.1% in June**, within  the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas’ (BSP) forecast range of 5.6 - 6.4%  for the month. ==Year-to-date, inflation averaged 4.7% in the first  seven months of the year==. **Food price increase of 7.1% YoY  stemmed from the rise in meat and seafood prices.**  ==The continued surge in oil prices and the weakness of the Philippine peso (PhP) also contributed to the elevated inflation figure.==  As of August 31, the PhP further depreciated against  US dollar (USD) by 2.20% month-on-month closing at PhP 56.95.
- **BSP raised its key policy rate by 50 basis points (bps) to 3.75% amid  surging inflation.** Year-to-date, the central bank has raised rates  by a total of 175 bps this year but still signals further rate increases.  ==BSP Governor Medalla earlier assured the economy is strong enough to absorb rate hikes implemented.== Overnight deposit and lending facility rates were also hiked by 75bps to 2.75% and 3.75%,  respectively. The BSP raised its inflation outlook for 2022 to 5.4%  from 5.0% and lowered the 2023 projection to 4.0% from 4.2%,  as well as for 2024 to 3.2% from 3.3%.
