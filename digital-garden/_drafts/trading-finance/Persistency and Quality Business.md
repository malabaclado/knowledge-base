
> [!Note]
> Persistency rate is a measure of your quality business.

**Study period**: 13 months (2 annuals/3 semi-annuals/5 quarters/13 months)

**Components for calculation**
- API Issued - New business
- API Endorsements - Upgrades/Downgrades
- API FY Reinstatements - Plans who lapsed but are resinstated during the first year.
- API FY Lapse - Plans that lapsed during the first year.

**Formula**
$$\text{Persistency \%}=\frac{(\text{API Issued } \pm \text{API Endorsements } + \text{API FY Reinstatements } - \text{API FY Lapsed})}{\text{API Issued} \pm \text{API Endorsements}}=$$
## Benefits of High Persistency Rating
- Eligibility to persistency bonuses (MCPB, QPB)
- Participation in sales drives (travels & commissions)
- Renewal sales commissions

## Notes
- Standard persistency rate in PRU is 85%.
- Limited pay plans that are on "unit-cancellation" are considered as lapsed.
- Agents who adopt orphans are not subject to persistency risk
	- To maximize orphan clients: Upsell
- PA, PRUShield, PRUWellness, PRUDengue and Single Pay VUL are not included in agent's persistency rate calculations.
- Process of reinstatement takes 7 to 10 working days.
- Requirements for downgrading: (1) Policy Amendment Form; (2) Quotation Proposal
- You also need to consider the processing days (for change of frequency and downgrade)
	- For change of frequency: at least two weeks before the due of grace period.
	- For downgrades: at least two weeks before the next due date (not the grace period)
- Why surrender? If may existing lapsed policy, hindi na papayagan ulit kasi may existing lapse. Only option is to reinstate.
- Revalidation: 
	- Persistency: 2 months after contest period.

## How to avoid low persistency rating
1. Use the 60-day grace period. (1 month for Elite plans - on 31st day, may Premium Holiday Charge)
	- Remind clients to pay their premiums.
	- **Tip:** Just tell them that this is a 30-day grace period. This could be an "extra-mile".
2. Recommend options for change of frequency
	- Example: If semi-annual, recommend change to monthly for lower premium.
3. Recommend downgrade of premium
	1. Change of plan (during cooling-off period)
	2. Surrender the policy (during cooling-off period)
4. If the policy has fund value (esp PAA, Elite), switch to cash basis and quarterly.
	1. Note: Monthly is not available for cash basis.
	2. This option is more advantageous to the client.
5. Surrender the policy within 60-day grace period. Required signed full withdrawal form.
6. Use the cooling off period. (Applicable only if on the first month) Use the cooling-off form.
