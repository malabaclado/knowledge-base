# Compilation of Stock Market Price Dataset
- [Philippine Stock Exchange OHLC data for 2018](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/zealjeung/philippine-stock-exchange-ohlc-data-for-2018)
- [Philippine Stock Exchange Dataset (PSE)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shanemaglangit/philippines-stock-exchange-dataset)
	- This dataset contains the historical data of 303 publicly listed companies in the Philippines Stock Exchange from December 2011 to March 24, 2021. All data were scraped from [https://ph.investing.com](https://ph.investing.com/).

- [META | Stock Market](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/whenamancodes/meta-stock-market-analysis-founding-years)
- [Amazon | Stock Market](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/whenamancodes/amazon-stock-market-analysis-founding-years)
