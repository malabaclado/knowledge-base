**data science is both rare and high demand**
rare quality = data science takes unstructured data, then finds order, meaning & value
high demand = data science provides insight and competitive advantage

**what do data scientists do?**
- coding - used to gather data
	- stats coding (python/r)
	- database coding (sql)
	- command line (bash)
	- search (regex)
- math - helps choose the procedures and diagnose problems
- domain - expertise in field

**my ds position**: math graduate who can code and has financial domain knowledge

**why math matters in ds?**
1. know which procedures to do and why
2. know what to do when things don't go right
3. some math is easier and quicker to do by hand than by compute

**mathematics needed for data science** (what to review?)
- algebra
	- elementary algebra
	- linear algebra
	- systems of linear equations
- little bit of
	- calculus
		- basis in least squares regression and probability distributions
		- measuring change or rates of change
		- finding local maxima/minima
	- big O
	- probability theory
	- Bayesian statistics
