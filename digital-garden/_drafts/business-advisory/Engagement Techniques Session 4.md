# How to engage Level B
Goal: 
- Prime them - via content - to be open to meeting you for a financial/insurance planning session.
- Try to set up an appointment

What to do:
- Address lack of kowledge 
- Note limiting beliefs
- Correct misconceptions
- Respond to Objections

Close deliberately 
- Examples:
	- If parent: *tinatawag na ako ni bunso*
	- *Will make dinner first*
	- *Balikan kita sa/kapag*
	- *Huy, it's nice to catch up with you*

BEFORE: Clients meet you to see if they like you.
NOW: Clients should like you first before they meet you.

> *One step at a time, there's no need to rush* - Jordan Sparks
