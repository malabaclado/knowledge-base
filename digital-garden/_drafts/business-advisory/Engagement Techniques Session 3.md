Bad Practices
1. Don't talk about insurance for C and D level. 
2. Don't ask questions - D 
	1. ❌ May insurance ka na ba? 
	2. ❌ Insured na ba family mo?
	3. Don't give the impression na pakelamera ka or what. 
3. Don't chat late at night 
	1. ❌ Latest should be around 9 PM 
4. No conversation opener 
5. No appropriate conversation closing 

Inappropriate responses 
1. Showing neutral or uninterested tone
2. Giving unsolicited opinion and advice
3. Unsending/deleting a message

Inappropriate interactions
1. Late chat reply
2. No emoji/ Wrong use of emojis
3. Wrong timing

Negative expressions
1. Asking ver probing questions 
2. Pagsasabi na mali sila or sinungaling sila
3. Showing no empathy
4. Opening a negative topic

Best Practice: Conversations 
- Praise and compliment
	- Give sincere compliments 
	- Conrgatulate/admire 
	- Show support (like/share content)
	- Greet on birthdays
- Add value
- Make it engaging 
	- Talk about their interests 
	- Ask open-ended questions (Except for categ D)
	- Share viral content

Chatting tips

- Pain - what thing challenges you?
- Value - why didyou set this goal?
