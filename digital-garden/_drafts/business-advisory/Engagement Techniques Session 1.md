Engagement Techniques is a 4-module session. This program aims to develop skills and behavior in nurturing prospects.

Program objectives:
1. Learn about PRULeads System
2. Know how to encode and classify prospects according to category

# The DCBA System
The DCBA system is an alternative prospecting system to Hot-Warm-Cold system which focuses on your level of relationship to the client.


![](https://i.imgur.com/zGlKXx8.png)

The level of relationship allows us to know how to engage/what activities to do with this prospect.

# Engagement Techniques Framework ^519a25
1. CREATE. Create a list of 300-500 prospects
2. CATEGORIZE. Determine the level of relationship of each prospect
3. ENGAGE. Regularly engage the prospect based on the relationship
4. MAINTAIN. Manage relationship (Encode activities in the PRULeads app)


# 50-day Building Prospect List Period
The first 50 days are critical when looking for prospects. 


![](https://i.imgur.com/qFEgUVp.png)

# Engagements based on relationship
- Level D - Strangers
	- Freq: Every 8 weeks
	- Main goal: Build familiarity
	- Kamustahan
		- Hi/Hello
	- Sharing of articles/webinars
	- Greetings - birthdays, anniversaries, achievements 
	- Interests 
		- *"Mahilig ka pala sa kdrama?"* *"Ano mga marerecommend mo?"*
		- Look for your things in common.
	- #idea Send articles from your blog - make one!
- Level C - Small talk
	- Freq: Every 6 weeks
	- Main goal: Build relationship
- Level B - More personal talk
	- Freq: Every 3 weeks
	- Goal: Maintain relationship
- Level A - Supports you
	- Freq: Every week
	- Goal: Approach for proposal
