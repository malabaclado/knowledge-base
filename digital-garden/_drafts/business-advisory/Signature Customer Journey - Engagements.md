# Signature Customer Journey - Engagements Notes

Based on study on lapsed policies, 82% of them lapse within one year from date of issuance. Reason: Di na nakakareceive ng attention or service from their agents.

Pag hindi nakikita ni client yung importance and benefits of their policy, nakakalimutan nila (nawawala yung value).

> [!NOTE]
> Key Questions:
> 1. What is your idea of engagement and nurturing existing clients?
> 2. What are the benefits of nurturing existing customers?
