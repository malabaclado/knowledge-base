## Philosophy
Our brains are powerful processors, but it's not entirely made of unlimited resource. Therefore it is best used for higher level tasks -- processing, making ideas, making sense of concepts and creating systems.


Here comes the second brain: we use it to automate, store and hasten our processes --such as storage of concepts, for faster linking and unravelling unlikely connections.

> _Building A Second Brain is a methodology for saving and reminding u of the ideas, inspirations, insights, and connections we’ve gained through our experience. It expands our memory and our intellect using the modern tools of technology and networks._ – Tiago Forte
