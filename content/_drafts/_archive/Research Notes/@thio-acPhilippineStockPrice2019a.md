# Philippine Stock Price Forecasting Using Artificial Neural Network

> [!info]
> - **Cite Key:** [[@thio-acPhilippineStockPrice2019a]]
> - **Abstract:** In this study, MATLAB's artificial neural network was used to efficiently forecast a stock price for the following day. The decision support system made use of the software's Financial Toolbox which contains different technical indicators. The five indicators used are Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD), Relative Strength Index (RSI), Bollinger Bands, On-Balance Volume (OBV) and Stochastic. The data of the past stock prices are analyzed by the time series artificial neural network using a Nonlinear Autoregressive with External (Exogenous) Input (NARX) type of problem which uses previous outputs as input for future predictions, to give a forecast. The system utilized the historical stock prices of the companies containing at least 15 years of data, 70% for training, 15% for validation, and 15% for testing in the neural network. The system includes a Decision Support System (DSS) which provides an overall decision whether to buy, hold or sell shares on a certain Philippine Stock Exchange-listed (PSE) company. Based on the results, the program will generate a report in a PDF file. Overall, this study provides an accuracy of 98.08% for the forecast of Company A's neural networks, 97.91% for the forecast of Company C's neural networks, 97.10% for the forecast of Company F's neural networks, 97.85% for the forecast of Company H's neural networks and 98.10% for the forecast of Company I's neural networks. The predicted high and low prices for the five companies are proven accepted as tested using t-test with a 5% level of significance.

- I. INTRODUCTION

- Less than one percent of the population participate in stock market investment compared to Hong Kong, Malaysia, and Singapore which represents 35.7%, 12.5%, and 12% of their populations, respectively. One factor which affects the low investment population of the country is the incidence of poverty meaning people do not have enough money to invest.

- Stock market is a financial investment which can create excessive capital though it has a volatile nature making it a “very high-risk investment” [2]. It is an environment where company owners and common people do transactions, benefitting both sides, growing mutually. Company owners sell shares of their company to the public, using their money as financial capital and as the company expand and generate income, the shareholders receive dividends, giving them power to buy more shares or just spend it any way they can.

- Prediction of stock prices is a common challenge for financial researchers. Many use various data and bases to predict its change, even gut-feel and instinct. However, the uncertainty and nonlinear behavior in stock market trends make it difficult even for financial experts to make accurate predictions. Predicting stock price is a “challenging task from financial time series forecasting” since stock market is known to be dynamic, complex, non-linear and non- parametric [3].

- The artificial neural network (ANN), an artificial intelligence method, is a computing framework that can be used to an extensive range of time series projecting problems with a high degree of accuracy. Complex realtime data can be learned to be interpreted effectively by artificial neural networks. Recent studies demonstrated the great ability of neural networks in solving problems related to non-linear time series [4]. The study employs a time series neural network with Nonlinear Autoregressive with External Input (NARX) as type of problem. The external inputs in this case are the other parameters included in the historical price of a company. This type is more efficient than others since it uses the previous outputs as inputs for the future forecasts.

- The development of a system capable of forecasting the next day’s stock price through artificial neural network and generating a suggested decision was successfully implemented using MATLAB. Also, a program based on Visual Studio 2012 which includes basic stocks tutorial was made and successfully interfaced with the stock price prediction and decision support system.

- It was found out that the accuracies of the forecasted high and low prices of the five companies range from 96.97482% to 98.63325%. The predicted high and low prices for the five companies are accepted as tested using t-test with a 5% level of significance.

- The study was successfully implemented and done; however, additional external inputs that affect the price of stocks will be included, (2) additional companies for the expansion of the project must be added (3) and an artificial neural network with smaller mean square errors must be developed to lessen the percent error of the forecasts.
