# Guide to engagement and nurturing of existing clients

Engagement tips:
- Greetings and congratulations on life events
- Create a facebook page for more info dissemination
- Invite them to webinars
- Constant bite-sized engagements
- Do a policy anniversary
	- Create policy anniversary tracker/journal 
	- Possible create a profile on obsidian
	- Personalized gift w/ message every anniversary - for their commitment
	- Keep client records on Excel and reminders on TickTick
	- Use PRULeads and policy anniversary guide
