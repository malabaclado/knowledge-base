**Analytics** progresses data along its value chain, with the purpose of delivering the right decision support to the right people and digital processes at the right time, for the greater good.

# Analytics as Decision Support
Analytics as a decision support means that **while analytics can provide the end used (the stakeholders) with data, information, insights and prescribed actions, we should maintain that the end user can still choose to act upon them or not**. The final decision is still with the end user.

# Analytics Terminologies
- The 'data' stage is commonly linked to these terms: 
	- Data governance
	- Data management
	- Data security
	- Data ethics
- The 'information' stage is commonly linked to these terms:
	- Data engineering 
	- Data warehousing 
	- Data architecture
	- Business intelligence
	- Descriptive Analytics
- The 'insights' stage is commonly linked to these terms:
	- Data mining
	- Algorithms
	- Machine learning
	- Diagnostic analytics
	- Predictive analytics
- The 'imperatives' stage is commonly linked to these terms:
	- Optimization
	- Simulation
	- Recommendation engines
