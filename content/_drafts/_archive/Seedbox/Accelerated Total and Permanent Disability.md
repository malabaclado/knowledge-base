# Accelerated Total and Permanent Disability
This is a core benefit in common VUL plans.


![[Pasted image 20220614183308.png]]


Criteria for Total and Permanent Disability 
- If working: cannot go to work and earn for 6 consecutive months
- If not working: cannot do 3 out of 6 daily activities for 6 months.
	- Transferability - hindi makatayo
	- Bathing  - di makaligo
	- Dressing - di makapagdamit
	- Mobility - di makagalaw
	- Continence - di na makaihi/makadumi
	- Feeding - di makakain
