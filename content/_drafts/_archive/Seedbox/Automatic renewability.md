# Automatic renewability
If at the end of the current benefit term of this policy and every year thereafter, the Life Insured has not reached age ninety (90), this policy may be renewed annually, provided that: 
- the policy is **renewed at the same sum assured** as shown in the Policy Data Page less any accelerated benefits already paid; and 
- the attained age of the life insured at the end of each benefit term is **not more than eighty-nine (89)**; Premiums on the renewed policy shall be based on the attained age as of last birthday of the life insured at the date of renewal.
