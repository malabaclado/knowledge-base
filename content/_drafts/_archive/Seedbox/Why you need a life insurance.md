# Why you need a life insurance
1.  Guaranteed Protection
	- If you have a family, a business, or others who depend on you, the life insurance benefit of a whole life policy acts as a financial safety net.
2. Income Replacement
3. Tax-free benefit
4. Cash value growth
5. Dividend potential
6. Customizable riders
7. Create a source of savings

## 3 reasons to purchase life insurance when you are in your 20s.
**It can help pay for life’s expenses.**
**It can grow wealth over time.**
**The earlier you buy the better.**

## 7 reasons you may need life insurance, even if you think you don't

**1. You're going to have a baby or already have children**
**2. You're planning to get married**
**3. You support aging parents financially**
**4. You have private student loan debt**
**5. You work for yourself or have a family-owned business**
**6. Your job is high-risk**
**7. You have extreme hobbies**



PROS AND CONS
Pros
1. Tax-deferred growth
2. Lifetime coverage
3. Can borrow from your cash value
4. Accelerated benefits

Cons
1. Payment of premium



-   Term life insurance can make sense if you want to be covered for a set time period, while permanent life insurance can cover you for life.
- Whether or not life insurance is a good investment for you depends on your individual finances as well as the length you'll need coverage.
