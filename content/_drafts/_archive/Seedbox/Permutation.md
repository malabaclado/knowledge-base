# Permutation

Definition. Let $A= a_{1},a_{2},...,a_{n}$ be a set of $n$ distinct objects.
- If $0 \leq r \leq n$, then an r-permutation of A is an arrangement of any $r$ of the objects of A in a row.
- Ir $r=n$, an r-permutation of A is simply called a permutation of A.
