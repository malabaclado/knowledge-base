FAQ 
- Can investors switch funds? Yes as long as same currency 
- Saan nakikita ang price per day? May link po, Viber community and Email on a daily basis 
- If full redemption is made, would the account automatically close? No. It would still be open for a period of time. 
- Where to get historical data? Email Pru Life UK Investments 
- How to invest if historical performance is negative? When the market is down, it is an opportunity to invest in the market. 
- Pwede po ba mag-invest ang companies? Yes. Iba lang ang form. Email PAMTC for account opening forms. 
- Pwede ba to follow bak account ni client? No. Required siya sa opening.
- If dollar account can it be a foreign bank? No. It has to be a local bank. 
-  Can filipinos abroad invest? At the moment, no. 
- Can we ask for PDF copy of account opening forms? Yes. Email lang sila.
- What happens if primary investor dies? It will be part of estate of the deceased, subject to estate tax. 
- If client has VUL, need pa po ba mag-open ng account for UITFs? Yes. Iba po kasi talaga. 
- Are we allowed to share market watch? YES 
- Most invested fund? Peso Liquid, Index Tracker. 
- Can clients set beneficiaries? Wala po. Diretso na po sa estate tax.
