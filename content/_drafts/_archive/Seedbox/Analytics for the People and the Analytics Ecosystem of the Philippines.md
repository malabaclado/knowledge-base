- # Designing for Digital
	- "*One cannot really separate digital transformation with analytics as it is fundamental to how organizations can compete in the digital age.*"
	- Dr. Ginny Ross asks organizations to make four key decisions as they design for the digital age.
- # Four Key Decisions
	- What is our vision for improving the lives of our customers?
	- What is our strategic driver: market-driven through customer engagement, or product-driven through digitized solutions?
	- What are our key digital capabilities?
	- How will we architect our business?
- *Digital transformation is not about technology, it's about the people.*
