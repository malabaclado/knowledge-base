# Multiple Life Care Plus
![[Pasted image 20220614193252.png]]
Can claim up to 3 critical illness claims provided:
- The critical illnesses belong to different groups 
- At least 1 year interval between claims. 

2 CI claims can be filed from Group 1 Cancer provided that: 
- 2nd claim is made 5 yrs of cancer free period. 
- 2nd cancer claim can be a re-occurence of 1st claim.
