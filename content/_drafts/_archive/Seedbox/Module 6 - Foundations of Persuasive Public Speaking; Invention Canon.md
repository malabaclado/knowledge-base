**Five canons of rhetoric**
Roman educators formulated the canons of rhetoric to serve as a set of standards or guide to develop a well-crafted speech. 

1. [[Invention (Inventio)]] 
2. [[Disposition (Dispositio)]]
3. [[Style (Elocutio)]]
4. [[Memory (Memoria)]]
5. [[Delivery (Pronunciato)]] - use of language, voice, and nonverbal cues in oral discourse.

**Modes of delivery**
- Impromptu - This spur of the moment speech involves very little (a couple of minutes) to no preparation.
- Manuscript - The whole speech is written and read word for word in front of an audience. 
- Memorized - The whole speech is written, committed to memory, and recited in front of your audience.
- Extemporaneous - Thorough preparation is required, which includes crafting an outline (disposition canon). Instead of memorizing the whole speech, you refer to your outline during delivery.
