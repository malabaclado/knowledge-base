- The speech came from an 11-minute video I watched from YouTube. (Link: [FULL SPEECH: Ping Lacson at campaign kick-off - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hwXMR2KuFRk))
- This is Presidential Candidate Ping Lacson's speech at campaign kick-off last February 8 at the proclamation rally of his candidacy at Imus Granstand, Imus, Cavite.
- The audience are mostly Cavitenos along with supporters of Lacson.

In this speech, Presidential Candidate Ping Lacson

---

Ethos: 
- Demonstrated by his good posture, strong voice and facial expressions.
- Demonstrated by first acknowledging the audience and giving praises to his vice-presidentiable.
- Experience: Military, Police and as a Senator
- "Ni minsan hindi kami tumanggap ng suhol kapalit ng serbisyo publiko"
- "Bilang lehitimong kabitenyo. Hinding hindi ko kayo bibiguin"

Pathos
- Isipin natin ang kinabukasan ng ating mga anak, ang susunod na henerasyon. "Ito ba ang gusto nating ipamana? Ngayon palang parang humingi na tayo ng tawad sa kanila"
- Kapag nag-aabroad tapos pauwi na sasabihin "Back to reality nanaman"
- Kapag hindi tayo pumili ng tamang iboboto, magsusuffer tayo.

Logos
- Dalawang magnanakaw: namimili ng ninanakawan, pinipili ng ninanakawan. Wag bumoto ng magnanakaw.
- NO 1 problem ng bansa: Gobyerno. Solusyon: Good governance: **"Ayusin ang gobyerno para maging maayos ang buhay ng Pilipino"**
