# Girard Desargues
Wiki Page: [Girard Desargues - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girard_Desargues)


> **Girard Desargues** (21 February 1591 – September 1661) was a French [mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematician "Mathematician") and engineer, who is considered one of the founders of [[Projective Geometry]]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_geometry "[[Projective Geometry]]").^[[Girard Desargues - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girard_Desargues)]

He is known as the Father of [[Projective Geometry]]
