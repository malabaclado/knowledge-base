# Fund Switch
![[Pasted image 20220616201625.png]]
- **Fund switch** is transferring a fraction or whole fund value of a fund to another fund due to change of life stage or risk profile. 
- Fund switches are subject to a fund switch charge of 0.5% of the switch amount. (Except when there are free switches) 
- **When is the best time to switch funds?** To avoid further loses
