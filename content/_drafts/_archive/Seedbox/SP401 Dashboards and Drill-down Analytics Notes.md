# Module 4. Drill-down Analytics
Drill-down analysis
- Is the process of breaking down an overall performance, or a large, complex business problem into finer components.

Drill-down tasks you can do with pivot table:
1. Break down performance with respect to a KPI by contribution source (store, product)
2. Analyze overall trend and influence of seasonality (eg. weekday, month, quarter)
3. Investigate the effects of special events (e.g. promotions)
