# Axioms of Projetive Geometry
![[Projective Geometry#^95134e]]
**The 4 Basic Axioms**

P1: Two distinct points lie on a unique line 
P2: Two distinct lines pass through a common point 
P3: A line has at least three points 
P4: There exists three noncollinear points 


**Theorem** Consequence of 4 Basic Axioms 
T1: $\Pi$ is non-empty. *(Follows from P4)*
T2: Two distinct lines have exactly one point in common. *(Dual of P1, Proof by contradiction)*
T3: A point lies in at least three lines. *(Dual of P3)*
T4: There exists three nonconcurrent lines. *(Dual of P4)*
