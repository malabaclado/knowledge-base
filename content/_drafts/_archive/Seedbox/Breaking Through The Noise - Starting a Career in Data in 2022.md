# Breaking Through The Noise - Starting a Career in Data in 2022

This is a webinar from DataCamp. 

---
**How do you stand out??**
- It is projected that there will be 2.7 million jobs in data and analtics in 2022
- One problem you might be the plethora of job titles in this career. 


![[Pasted image 20220623215816.png]]


How do you break throught the noise?
1. **Know your why**. 
	- Why do you wanna work in the data career? This helps you narrow your focus on your objectives.
	- Exercise:
		-  Write out a vision statement.
		-  Write out your perfect day at work. How do you visualize it will go? What type of problems are you solving?
2. **Know yourself**
	- As you look for jobs, you need to present yourself. 
	- If you want to stand out from the crowd, you have to be yourself.
	- Do this exercise:
		- Take the 16 personalities test. This is a good starting point to know your strengths and wewaknesses.
		- Map your experience. People always think they're starting from zero, but that's not always the case. 
		- Write your current skills. Write your target job skills. ![[Pasted image 20220623220340.png]]
3. **Connect** 
	- Your network = your net worth 
	- Community is key. 
	- Who you surround yourself with are the ones who will propel you into action.
