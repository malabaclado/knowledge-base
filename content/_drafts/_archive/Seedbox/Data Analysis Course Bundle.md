The RITx Data Analysis for Decision-Making Professional Certificate program consists of three courses:

- [[Data Literacy Foundations]]
- [[Data processing and analysis with excel]]
- [[Data representation and visualization in Tableau]]

These three courses take you through a six-stage data literacy development process that begins with comprehending what data is through to communicating a data story to make and gain support for decisions.
