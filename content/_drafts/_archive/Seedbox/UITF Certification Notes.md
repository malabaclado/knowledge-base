## Zooming in on UITFs
UITF are *open-ended pooled trust fund* in *any acceptable currency* *operated by a trust entity* and *made available by participation*

- open-ended: no maturity date, can withdraw at any time 
- acceptable currency in PAMTC are Peso or Dollar denominations 
- Trust entity: PAMTC 
- available by participation: by buying units
