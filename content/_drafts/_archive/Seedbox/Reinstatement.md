# Reinstatement
- If the policy terminates due to non-payment of premiums, the client may apply to reinstate it **within five (5) years from the due date of the premium** in default if he or she gives the Company satisfactory evidence of good health of the life insured at his or her own expense. 
- He or she can start paying the current premium due **without paying any of the overdue premiums.** 
- To apply, the client must use our appropriate application form and meet the conditions stipulated on it. The company will notify him or her if the application is accepted or rejected.
