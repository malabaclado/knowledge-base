# Supporting Facts
-   Annual family income is estimated at PhP 313 thousand, on average, in 2018. A family’s main income earner would need at least 10 to 12 times of his or her annual income to replace family income loss.
-   1 out of 10 Filipinos are out of school youths (OSY)—30% of them drop out because of family and financial struggles.
-   In 2018, residential property prices in NCR increased 6.8% while in areas outside the NCR, prices rose by 2.2%.
