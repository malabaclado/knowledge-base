# Survival Models
We'll look at mathematical models of life and life expectancy. 

**Survival Function**
The survival function is the probability that $(x)$ will survive the next $t$ years. 

---
*Conditions for a valid survival function*
1. $S_{x}(0)=1$, the probability of survival at present is 1. 
2. $\lim_{t \to \infty} S_{x}(t)=0$. That is, all loves eventually die.
3. The survival function is non-increasing function.
