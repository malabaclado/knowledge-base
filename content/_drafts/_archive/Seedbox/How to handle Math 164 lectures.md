# How to handle Math Lectures
==It is important to have quality notes.==

The lecture runs for 3 hours. 

Divide the topic into 2. At the middle of the lecture, you may cut or take a break.

After the first half of the lecture, make sure you have written quality notes.

###### Note-taking markup
- **Bold important terms**. 
- ==Highlight thought that you have written for yourself. This will show your thinking what you think at the moment without cluttering your note.==



---

Handling Lectures:
- Use markup
	- Bold important terms
	- Highlight your own thoughts (Even lingering thoughts that might not be relevant.)
- During Lecture
	- Take notes of all important things
	- Take screenshots, links, etc
	- Take note of problems and exercises
- Post-lecture
	- Organize and re-markup
	- Add ideas to mindmap 
	- Add proofs/ proof synopses
	- make flashcards
- Next time
	- Do remaining tasks
