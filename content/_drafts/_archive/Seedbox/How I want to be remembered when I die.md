# How I want to be remembered when I die

**How I want to be remembered by my Family**
- As someone who loves them - someone who puts them first.
- Someone that is selfless.
- Someone that strives to be better everyday.
- Someone who is kind and generous.



**How I want to be remembered by my friends**
I want to be remembered as:
- Someone who inspires and motivates them.
- Someone who listens and is always there for them.
- Someone who always sees the best in people.
- A person who has a dream.
- A kind and generous person.
- Someone who has achieved his dream and happiness in life. 

**How I want to be remembered by my workmates (PruLife, UPMMC, DSC)**
I want to be remembered as:
- Someone who has made many contributions
- Someone who always does his best even in the worst situations
- Someone who values time and respect
- Someone who treats people equally
- Someone who's always willing to help other people 

**How I want to be remembered by the community (UPMMC, DSC, UP, Dasma)**
I want to be remembered as:
- Someone likeable.
- Someone who made contributions to the community.
- A person with integrity.
- A person who has achieved success.
- Someone important. 

--- 
**My core values and definition of success**
- Family as first priority. I want to be able to communicate to my mom and dad how much I love them. I want to live up to their expectations and I want to be able to give them the best things in life. 
- Kindness and Generosity
- Always strive to be better. Grit.
- Be an inspiration to others.
- See the best in every person. I want to motivate them and help them the same way that I worked for myself.
- I want to be always there for my friends when they need help. 
- I want to make many contributions to the causes and interests that I believe in. 
- I want to be persistent. I want to do my very best in all situations. 
- I value time and respect. 
- I want to treat every person equally. Remove discrimination and biases. 
- Always be ready to help other people. To do that, you have to help yourself first - so when its your turn to help them, you can.
- I want to be someone people would be attracted to. A person with honesty and integrity.
