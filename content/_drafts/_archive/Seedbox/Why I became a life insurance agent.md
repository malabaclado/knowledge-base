# Why I became a life insurance agent

WHY DID I BECAME A LIFE INSURANCE AGENT?
I wanted to talk to people. To hold conversations, to understand them and listen to their needs. With this, I hope to become a confident speaker. I hope to learn the best things to say at different situations. I hope to instill into me a sense of discipline and a sense of passion. 

Also, I want to be able to help them. I want to feel good by saying that I've had one life insured. Because every one life insured, is one life changed.
