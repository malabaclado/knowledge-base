We first studied how data moves along the [[Data Value Chain]]. Analytics refines data to extract its value as it transforms data into information, to insights and to imperatives.

We learned the definition of [[Analytics (Topic)|analytics]], what it means for analytics to be a decision support, and some terminologies related to it.

We looked at the [[Major Analytics Job Families]]. Then we talked about [[APEC Project DARE]] and the [[APEC Analytics Competencies]] as well as the [[AAP Professional Maturity Model]] which can be used to craft your own roadmap.

We also talked about [[DELTA+ Model and the Organizational Maturity Model]] and [[Organizational Strategy Road Map for Analytics]].

Finally, we discussed about [[Analytics for the People and the Analytics Ecosystem of the Philippines]].
