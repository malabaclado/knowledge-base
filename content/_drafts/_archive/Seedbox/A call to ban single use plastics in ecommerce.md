# A call to climate action

**Narrowing the topic:**
➡️ A call to climate action 
➡️ A call to decrease the use of plastics 
➡️ A call to decrease the use of plastics in online selling

**Working Title:**
- E-commerce and plastic pollution: How to mitigate the effects of buying and selling online to the environment.
- Why we should use less plastics in product packaging and how to do it.
- Why we should -- for a more eco-friendly e-commerce and how to do it. 
- Why we should talk more about environmental sustainability in e-commerce. 
- Why we need more discourse on environmental sustainability in e-commerce. 
  
**Goal:**
- Discourage the use of single-use plastics 
- Encourage more conversation on environmentally sustainable practices in e-commerce. 
- Encourage a more environmentally sustainable practice in the use of plastics in e-commerce. 


Audience:
- Speech 30 classmates 
- Business-owners 
- General public - that participates in an e-commerce activity
- Government

## Step 7: Formulate your introduction and conclusion

A well-crafted introduction serves the following purposes:

-   Gain the attention of your audience and motivate them to listen  
    
-   Establish rapport or connection with your audience  
    
-   Establish your ethos as a speaker, your knowledge, experience, interest on the issue  
    
-   State your claim and preview your main points  
    

Your introduction should be brief (less than 20 seconds) so as not to take away time that should be allotted for the meat of your speech — your main ideas and supporting evidence.

If your introduction serves as a preview of your main ideas, your conclusion serves as closure. Make sure that you do not provide new information in your conclusion. Simply summarize your main points and reiterate your claim.
