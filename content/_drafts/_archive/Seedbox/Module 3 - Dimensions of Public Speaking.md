# Module 3 - Dimensions of Public Speaking
> While there are many ways to express our sentiments using both written and oral language, our words are most impactful when they successfully make others “see the world” from our perspective, or at the very least, consider our viewpoint.

> The primary aim of all education is to form the habit of thinking, of judging facts and circumstances in their proper light, of logically deducing inferences from them — and this aim cannot be attained save through the instrumentality of a liberal education.^[President Rafael Palma’s inaugural address as UP President in 1925]

> “effectual democratic citizenship depends upon speaking subjects capable of entering into public acts of intersubjective discourse and meaning-making, who are resilient in the face of acts of disrespect based upon how they speak rather than what they stand for".^[Coleman (2014)]

**Rhetoric** is communication that influences the attitudes or behaviors of others; it is also called the *art of persuasion*.
