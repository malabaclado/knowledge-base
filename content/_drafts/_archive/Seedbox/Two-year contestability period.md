# Two-year contestability period
The two-year contestability period will apply if claims are filed within the policy’s first two years. This means that the claims will have to undergo investigation before the benefit is given.

If Pru Life UK denies a claim due to suicide (not insanity), concealment, or material misrepresentation, they will return the amount of premiums paid.
