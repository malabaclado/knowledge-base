# The Distribution Function of Future Lifetime RV
- Denotes the probability that a person of age *x* will die in the next *t* years.
$$\begin{align*}
F_{T_{X}}(t) &= F_{X}(t)\\
& =\mathbb{P}[T_{X}\leq t]\\
& = \space_{t}q_{x} 
\end{align*}$$
- It is also called the *lifetime distribution* from age x.
---
Associated with the continuous random variable $T_{x}$ is its distribution function which denotes the probability that a person of age $(x)$ dies within the next $t$ years.

## Relation to Survival Function
The complement of the distribution function called the [[Survival Function]].
$$S_{x}(t) = 1 - F_{x}(t)$$
The Distribution Function $F_{x}(t)$ denotes the probability of death. 
The [[Survival Function]] denotes the probability of survival.

---

## Notation

- $\space_{t}p_{x}$ ➡️ probability that a person of age $x$ survives in the next $t$ years.
- $\space_{t}q_{x}$ ➡️ probability that a person of age $x$ dies in the next $t$ years.
- $\space_{u|t}q_{x}$ ➡️ probability that a person of age x survives in the next $u$ years and dies in the next $t$ years after that.
