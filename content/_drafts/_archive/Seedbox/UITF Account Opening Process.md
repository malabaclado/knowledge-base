Notes:
- If no TIN number because never worked, OFW or student, perdeng NO TIN NUMBER, but OTHER ID Number is still required. 
- I think its better to guide your client with answering the Account Opening Form. 
- Starting today, hindi na accepted ang old account opening forms.
- TIP. Use Excel on laptop to prevent issues. 
- 
  
On Account Opening
- If complete and narecieve before 1:30 PM, maioopen naman kaagad. 
- If client is CIA, the KYC photo is done with the IC. 
- Up to 4 people ang joint account. 
- Subject: "`FAO: <initials of client>`"


Notes on AML 
- You may leave the Prospect No. blank 
- Required ang "expected transactions"
- AML ay kailangan lang upon account opening. 

**Question** ::❓How to switch funds? Can it be done on the same day?  *Answer: Redeem-subscribe process. Hindi pwede same-day. Dapat maclear muna yung fund from previous fund bago maibigay sa nw fund. Nag-aapply ang holding period.*

**Question** ::❓Pwede ba mag-open ang minor? *Answer: No. Dapat legal age (18+)*
