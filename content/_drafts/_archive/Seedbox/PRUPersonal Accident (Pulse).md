# PRUPersonal Accident (Pulse)

*This page is about the standalone Personal Accident product in Pulse. For the personal accident rider, see [[Personal Accident Rider]].*

---

**PRUPersonal Accident (PA)** is a yearly renewable packaged protection plan designed to cover injury or death due to accident.

## Benefits

![[Pasted image 20220312204839.png]]


### Accidental Total and Permanent Disability Benefit
- Cash benefit

### Murder and Assault Benefit
If the insured dies due to murder, one-half (1/2) of the benefits indicated in the schedule of losses not exceeding Php 1 million will be paid.

### Renewal Bonus
Increases the benefit amount of PA by 5% based on the original sum assured for up to maximum of five (5) years.

### Burial Benefit
Provides a cash benefit if the insured dies due to natural death.

### Hospital Income Benefit
Provides a daily cash benefit for each day of hospital confinement due to accident up to 30 days of continuous confinement per accident.

### Accidental Medical Expense Reimbursement Benefit
Reimburses inpatient and outpatient medical expenses incurred within thirty (30) days from the date of an Accident.

### Dangerous Sports Coverage 
 If the insured shall **incur bodily injuries or shall lose his life due to an accident** while participating **as an amateur** in dangerous sports, the company will pay the benefit.

## Exclusions
- ==Intentional self-inflicted injury and suicid==e (whether sane or insane)
- By war, riot, civil commotion, strike, rebellion, revolution and similar events.
- By weapon of nuclear nature and radiation.
- Congenital anomalies
- ==Pregnancy, miscarriage, abortion.==
- By cosmetic or plastic surgery, ==except as a result of injury.==
- While in active service in the armed forces.
- ==While in any attempted commission or, or participation in any crime or while resisting lawful arrest.==
- ==By medical and surgical treatment, except as may be necessary solely as a result of injury.==
- ==While under the influence of alcohol or drugs.==
- By disease, bacterial infection or any consequence arising from Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS) or AIDS Related Complex (ARC).
- ==By mental or nervous disorders.==
- ==By poison, gas or fumes involuntarily taken.==
- ==By dangerous sports, UNLESS covered by Dangerous Sports Coverage.==
- ==Injuries or death occassioned by the provocation of the insured.==
- ==Driving or riding a motorcycle for recreational purposes: motor cross racing, joy riding, cross country riding, etc.==

## Other Provisions
- Grace period of 31 days
- Contestability period of 2 years from date of effectivity or reinstatement.
- Effectivity date is upon payment of first premium.
- Company must be given a written notice of claim, including the Policy contract within 30 days after the date of Injury, and immediately in case of death. 
- ==Misstatement of Age==. If the age of the insured is misstated, the Sum Insured will be adjusted to the amount which the premium would have purchased at the correct age. 
- Beneficiary. The allowed beneficiaries are:
	- legal spouse and children
	- parents
	- brothers and sisters
	- real estate
- ==Changes in occupation must be reported.== Depending on the case, the company can either decrease the Sum Assred *(low risk to high risk change)* or decrease the premium *(high risk to low risk change)*.
- ==Disapperance.== If the insured disappeared **for one year,** as a result of sinking, or wrecking of aircraft or similar incidents, **it will be presumed that the insured suffered loss of life**. If after settlement of claims, the insured shall be found to be alive, all amounts so paid shall be refunded to the Company.
