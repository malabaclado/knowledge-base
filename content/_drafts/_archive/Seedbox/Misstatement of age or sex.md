# Misstatement of age or sex
If the age and/or sex of the life insured has been misstated, the sum assured will be adjusted to the amount which the premium will have purchased at the correct age or sex, applicable risk class, and applicable premium rate as of policy effectivity date, or as of the effectivity date of any increase in sum assured.
