# 220419 Handling Objections (Jeanette Hilot)
❓How do you convince a person (na may existing policies na) to let you review their policy kahit hindi po ikaw yung servicing agent nila?
- Itanong sa kanila kung ano yung benefits ng policy nila. (Usually di nila alam yan) > Then tanungin mo kung bakit siya kumuha ng insurance previously (You'd have some ideas from this) > Then position another purpose. (UM Mico)
- Ask their current benefits. Palagay mo, ano ba yung kulang dito?

- Depende talaga siya sa takbo ng market. How to convince them: Ask kung ano talaga yung priority ni client, then focus on that. 

---
**Formula for Overcoming Objections**: Right Attitude + Right Strategy
- Objections are opportunities to clarify underlying ideas. It's a sign of the prospect's interest! 

---
## The Right Strategy
- **Re-state to clarify**
	- *I want to make sure that I understand you correctly and what you are actually saying is...* 
	- This a way to show that you are listening and a chance for you to clarify if the client's objection is a genuine concern. 
- **Isolate** 
	- Technique for isolating is to reverse the objection. 
	- Examples
		- Budget: *If budget was not an issue, will you go ahead with this plan?*
		- Check with other companies: *If you didn't find the need to check with other companies, does this plan make sense?* 
		- My friend has bad experience: *If not for your friend's experience, would it make sense to but this plan?*
- **Answer** 
	- Feel, Felt, Found technique 
		- I understant how you feel. I also have clients who felt the same way, but later on, this is what they found. 
	- Tell a motivating story 
	- The Ben Franklin case: *If you fail to plan, then you're planning to fail.*
