	- わたしのさいふはおかねがありません。(My wallet have no money.)
	- *hint:* かう means "to buy"; ね means "price/cost"

### Verbs

- あそぶ -> to play; to spend an enjoyable time
	- no object, usually just location
	- if playing a game, use する: **game をする**。
	- had fun in "place": **placeであそびます**。

- おふろにはいる -> to take a bath
	- おふろ　-> structure where people bathe; はいる -> means "to enter"
	- Note that this verb literally means "to enter the おふろ" this is different to Filipino concept of taking a bath where we clean our bodies. This is more like taking a bath as in - like bathing in a hot spring. 
	- Do not use this verb to ask if Japanese people take a shower (in the morning), they might take a shower but not use the おふろ.
	- Trivia: Typical おふろ is about 40 degrees Celsius. And is only used at night, typically before sleeping. Recommended time is 15 minutes.

- つれていく -> to take someone (somewhere)
	- おかあさん　は　こども　を　はいしゃ　に　つれていく。
	*The mother took her child to the dentist*
	- Note: Even though you are going with this "someone", we do not use と in the sentence. Instead, we use を as in the direct object.

- シャワーをあびる -> taking a shower (ru-verb)
	- あびる -> means "to shower"

- たばこをすう -> to smoke a cigarette
	- すう -> means "to inhale"

- やすむ -> to rest; to be absent (from work)
	- ==わたしはいちじかんやすみます
		*I'd take a one hour break.*==
	- ==わたしは　きょう　Hapon 11をやすみます
		Today I'm absent from Hapon 11.==
		
- つかう -> to use
	==- コンピュータをつかいます
		*to use a computer*==
	
- かりむ -> to borrow (ru-verb)
	==- わたしは　ともだちに　かさを　かりました
		*I borrowed an umbrella from a friend.*==
	
	
- すわる -> to sit
	==- わたしは　テーブルに　すわりました。
		*I sat on the table.*==
		
- わすれる -> to forget
	==- パスワードを　わすれました。
		*I forgot my password.*==
	- To say you forgot to do something: (verb in dict form)の＋を＋わすれました。
		==- たべるの　を　わすれました
			*forgot to eat*==

- いそぐ -> to hurry
	- does not use a direct object

- てつだう -> to help/ to assist
	- ==メアリーさんはおかあさんをてつだいます。
		*Mary helps her mother*.==
		
- もってくる・もっていく-> bring (a thing) somewhere
	- もってきます　-> towards you (speaker)
	- もっていきます　-> away from you/ taking something to another place

- もち -> to hold/ to carry


- おしえる -> to teach / to divulge / to tell (something, typically a secret)
	- せんせいは　がくせいに　日本ごを　おしえます。
		*The teacher is teaching the student.*
		
	-  わたしは　ともだちに　でんわばんごうを　おしえました。
		*I told my friend my phone number.*
		
- しぬ -> to die

- たつ -> to stand

- はいる -> to enter/ to go inside

- かえす -> to return (a borrowed item)
	- Use: (person)に(object)をかえます。

- しめる -> to close (eg. door)
	- Use: (object)をしめます。
	- not used for "close your eyes"


- たける -> to open
- のる -> to ride
	- (vehicle)にのります。
	- (place)で(vehicle)にのります。
- おりる -> to unboard/ to leave (a vehicle)
	- (vehicle)をおります。
	- (place)で(vehicle)をおります

- つける -> to turn on (eg. lights)

- けす -> to turn off

- to make a call -> でんわをかける
	- (person)にでんわをかえります

- to answer a phone call →でんわにでます

- to recieve a phone call -> でんわをきます

- to erase -> けす
	- (object)をけします
