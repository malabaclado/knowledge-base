Radicals
- radicals are patterns and symbols that appear inside each kanji
- each kanji consists of 1–3 (sometimes four) individual radicals.

Radicals vs Strokes
- We tend to memorize more things when learning kanji by stroke than when learning them by radicals.

 Radicals + Mnemonics = Easy Kanji Memorizations
 - Know the radical of a kanji then make a story out of it (most easily based on how it is used).
