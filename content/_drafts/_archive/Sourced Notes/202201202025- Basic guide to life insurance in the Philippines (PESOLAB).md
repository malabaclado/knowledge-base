# Summary
> Insurance is a way for your family and beneficiaries to receive money on your untimely death.


## What are the different types of insurance
-   **Life insurance.** Also called death cover, it is a type of cover that leaves your family with money when you pass away. This includes among other kinds the [term insurance plan](https://pesolab.com/top-term-insurance-in-the-philippines-the-benefits-of-getting-insured-for-a-term/).
-   **[Health-maintenance organization (HMO)](https://pesolab.com/guide-to-hmo-health-insurance-in-the-philippines/).**  It is a health insurance that gives defined amount each year, called annual benefit limit, in time of sickness. It pays hospital bills, medicine, doctor’s fees, and even dental care.
-   **[Critical illness](http://critical%20illness/).** Also called trauma or critical illness cover, it provides lump sum amount to you and your family when you get sick of any ailments that are part of list of major diseases.
-   **Accident insurance.** An accident insurance is a policy that gives a certain amount of money when you sustain injuries due to accidents.
-   **Total and permanent disability.** It gives you money in time of disability, dismemberment and major burns.

## Life insurance
> Insurance PH plays an essential role to [personal financial planning](https://pesolab.com/essential-guide-to-financial-planning-for-pinoys/). It protects you against life’s uncertainties, such as sickness, disability and untimely death. It’s a means of replacing loss of income brought about by these circumstances, minimizing their impact on you and your family. And that’s why insurance is also called wealth or income protection.

## How to apply for life insurance?
> A life insurance is a contract, so the law requires that people getting one is at least 18 years old and literate.
-   Check insurance need
-   Pick the type of cover
-   Make a list of insurers
-   Set up an appointment with trusted advisor
-   Compare similar products before signing the policy
-   Prepare to sign the policy


## Frequently asked questions about life insurance
- Q: After acquiring life insurance, what should you do?
	- A: Keep electronic and hard copies of the policy, store them in a secure data drive or safe place, respectively.
	- Inform your beneficiaries. They should know the following:
		-   insurer
		-   details such as insurance product name, account number, etc.
		-   details such as coverage and exclusions
		-   location of the policy
		-   intended purpose of the proceeds such as end-of-life care and services, charity, etc.
		-   personal debts and other liabilities
		-   how you want them to spend their share of the proceeds



- Q: What are the reasons that your insurance application is denied?
	- A: Some of the reasons that you may be denied are old age, poor health, and risky occupation. For the full list of reasons, you may check this post that discusses [critical factors that affect insurability](https://pesolab.com/insurable-critical-factors-affect-ability-buy-insurance/).

- Q: What happens when a claim is denied?
	- A: According to *Insurance Commission*, you don’t have to go to court. They can hear out issues regarding policies for as long as the sum in dispute is less than 5 million pesos.
