# Ep 57 Glorious Return
Kim Dokja cleared the covenant scenario he made with the Secretive Plotter. As rewards, he was sent back to his world - the third round. Remember the last time he saw the party members was when he sacrificed himself to save his party members from the outer god. Now he's back! Since he technically came back from another world, he gained the status as a returnee. However, there is a twist! They triggered the returnee scenario. They were masked and the people of Earth thinks they are disasters. Kim Dokja looked like an 'Ugly Squid'. The scenario condition: they need to convince the people that who they are and leave a mark at the base in Seoul. He met and fought with his party members. He had a 1v1 with Yoo Junghyuk. They cleared the scenario by writing something on the wall of the industrial complex. They wrote 'Kim Dokja's Company' - the name of his nebula.
