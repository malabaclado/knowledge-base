## I. From Hermit to Sociable 
> Manners are the ability to put someone at their ease... by turning any answer into another question. - Tina Brown

1. **Use people's names.** Especially when they're with you.
2. **Address their partners, spouses, assistants and friends.** Not just the person you are talking to. Give everyone your equal and undivided attention.
3. **Use emphatizers.** 
	- Examples:
		- *I completely agree with you* 
		- *I see what you mean* 
		- *That's a lovely thing to say!* 
4. **Stand tall.** Always be aware of your posture amd how you carry yourself. 
5. **Give your undivided attention, body language included.**
	- How to do this:
		- Make direct eye contact. 
		- Turn your body to the one you're talking to. 
		- Nod in agreement.
		- Keep your body language *open* 
6. **Keep your hand gestures away from your face.**
	- hand in mouth or eyebrow - signals nervousness or dishonesty 
	- clasping head - signals frustration 
	- pinching nostrils - signals vexation 
7. **Dress for the occasion.**
8. **Do at least one good deed a day.** 
	- Doing a good deed makes us feel good about ourselves. This boosts our self-confidence.
9. **Ask questions that show you're interested.** 
	- Showing interest to another person makes them feel important.
	- Asking people about something they had just said signals that you **actually** listen. 
	- Just make sure you does not sound skeptic or interrogating. 
	- displaying suspicion and skepticism is alienating and divisice; being genuinely curious is a sign of charisma. 
10. **Focus first on the delivery, then on the words.** Delivery doesn't make your point different, but it will make sure that your point is more effective and clear.
	- Good content, coupled with great delivery, is an unbeatable combination.
11. **Have a variety of positive reactions, not the same generic smile for everyone.**
