# Division Algorithm for F[x]

> ==Theorem==
> Let F be a field. Let $f(x)=a_mx^m+,...,+a_0$ and $g(x)=b_nx^n+,...,b_0=$ be elements of F[x] with $n>0$ and $a_m,b_n \neq 0$ . There exists unique polynomials $q(x), r(x) \in F[x]$ such that $$f(x)=g(x)q(x)+r(x)$$ where $r(x)=0$ or $\text{deg } r< \text{deg } g =m$.
