# Image of a linear transformation
The image or range of a linear transformation $f$ is the set $$\text{Im}(f) = f(V) = \{f(v)|v \in V\}$$

Note that the image of $f$ is the set of all possible images of the elements of $V$. 

The image of a linear transformation is also a vector space itself. The dimension of $\text{Im}(f)$ is called its [[rank]].
