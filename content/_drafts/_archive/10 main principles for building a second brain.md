These ideas are sourced from Ali Abdaal's video on building a second brain.

1. **Borrowed creativity** 
	- Creations are not always original. Creativity is all about remixing stuff. 
2. **The capture habit**
	- Brains are for having ideas, not storing them. 
3. **Idea recycling**
	- recycling from the things you have already created 
4. **Projects over categories**
	- Think in projects, not in topics 
5. **Slow burns**: 
	- Doing things (esp projects) small rather than heavy-lifting. 
6. **Start with abundance** we start with our bank of knowledge, instead of a blank page
7. **Intermediate packets** drag and drop packets of content from one project to another (think writing an essay)
8. **You only know what you make** 
9. **Make it easier for your future self**
10. **Keep your ideas moving**
