
#### Redefining PARA

Projects - active tasks that are due very soon.
Areas - Wider categorization of projects; tracking areas of life.
Resources - Knowledge bank; place to easily review.
Archive - Completed and inactive projects, files and other materials that MAY BE USED in the future.


#### Application-PARA Analogy

Projects -> Ticktick
Areas -> Evernote
Resources -> Obsidian
Archive -> Google Drive/Backup Drive



#### Maintenance-PARA Analogy

Projects -> actionable on a very specific time
Areas -> to be reviewed on a weekly basis
Resources -> to be reviewed/maintained as needed
Archive -> completed and saved for future use

#### Example: Math Lectures

**Project**: Study Numerical Methods for ODEs
Success criterion: 
-	Read and annotate source material (Level 0 Groundnote)
-	Create flashcards (Spaced-Repetition)



**Area**
This is part of Math 171>Second Semester>Math Major. Once this is done, 


**Resources:**
Put the annotated source material (same name) into Obsidian. In here, you may do progressive summarization. This place is where an information can be easily retrieved and reviewed.

**Archive**
When I graduate, I should backup my notes (md) and files (pdf) to google drive.

#### Example: Literature Notes

Suppose I happen to read a good article.


**Prelimenary:** Save the article in Evernote

**Area**
Suppose I want to look at an area encompassing that article, what I want to do is:
- Read the article
- Create intended actions
	- Might be: change in habits, create flashcards, etc.
- Save the article in **Resources** under that **Area**
