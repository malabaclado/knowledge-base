# [250 Questions To Get To Know Someone & Spark Conversation](https://www.yourtango.com/2020334592/questions-ask-spark-conversation-get-know-someone)

Sometimes we all need a little help with keeping the conversation flowing. Whether you're on a date [or just meeting someone new](https://www.yourtango.com/experts/ronnieannryan/how-to-introduce-yourself), it's good to have questions to get to know someone on hand so you can break the ice and really get to the core of who someone is.

Meeting someone new can be difficult for introverts (and even extroverts!), especially [when it comes to small talk](https://www.yourtango.com/2016285381/real-reason-introverts-absolutely-hate-small-talk). There are so many factors that go into what’s discussed or when something is brought up. 

You have to take the social factors into consideration before conversating, because, as we all know, there are certain questions for certain times. 

## For example, if you are trying to [get to know someone on a date](https://www.yourtango.com/experts/dinazcolada/best-get-to-know-you-questions-to-ask-a-guy-girl-on-first-dates), you can play a game like 20 questions, where you go back and forth asking a question each turn. You can ask basic questions about likes and dislikes, dreams, and goals.

## Questions About Family To Get To Know Someone's Relatives:

Family drama can add pressure to relationships, so it's important to know where your date stands with their parents and other relatives. Are they close? Did they grow up in a loving environment? Questions about family members may be triggering, so respect what your date does and does not want to share.
