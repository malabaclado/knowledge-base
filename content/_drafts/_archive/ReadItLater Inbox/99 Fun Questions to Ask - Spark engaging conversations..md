# [99 Fun Questions to Ask - Spark engaging conversations.](https://www.mantelligence.com/fun-questions-to-ask/)

![Three friends looking at mobile phone - fun questions to ask](Three%20friends%20looking%20at%20mobile%20phone%20-%20fun%20questions%20to%20ask.jpg)

via: Depositphotos / Ivanko1980

Having **fun questions to ask** in your repertoire is a must!

Why do you need them?

**First impressions last.**

It’s why you you wear your best clothes to an interview, scrub every inch of your body before a [first date](https://www.mantelligence.com/first-date-tips-for-men/), and freak out before meeting her parents the first time.

The key to [making a great first impression](http://www.businessinsider.com/6-ways-successful-people-make-a-great-first-impression-2016-9)? Knowing fun questions to ask the people that you meet.

When you enter a novel situation with a stack of [interesting questions](https://www.mantelligence.com/interesting-questions/) to ask you **allow the people you meet to talk about their favorite subject: themselves.**

Asking a person questions about him or herself is an even better way to make a positive first impression than directing their focus on your own charm or sociability. Make use of these fun questions to [help new people open up](https://www.psychologytoday.com/blog/stronger-the-broken-places/201409/12-steps-getting-someone-open), feel comfortable, and walk away remembering how good you made them feel.

## 10 Fun Conversation Starting Questions to Ask

![Beautiful girl smiling with cropped friend](Beautiful%20girl%20smiling%20with%20cropped%20friend.jpeg)

via: Unsplash / Baylee Gramling

There’s nothing more awkward than meeting someone new and panicking to find something to talk about.

Small talk will last you a minute or two, but what you need at that moment are [conversation starters](https://www.mantelligence.com/conversation-starters/) that will lead you towards an fun, fascinating conversation. Before you know it you and your [new friend will feel at ease](https://www.fastcompany.com/3063609/8-ways-to-put-anyone-at-ease) and open, and the [conversation will flow naturally](https://www.mantelligence.com/how-to-keep-a-conversation-going/).

#### 1\. On a scale of 1-10, how strict were your parents?

On either end of the scale, parents are a great source of stories.

#### 2\. Who was your worst teacher? Why?

Bad teachers are the stuff of nightmares.

#### 3\. Who was your favorite teacher? Why?

Great teachers can change a student’s life forever.

#### 4\. What was your favorite toy growing up?

Childhood toys keep their sentimental value for life.

#### 5\. If you could time travel, when would you go?

First stop: wherever Mary McFly got his Nike’s.

![American astronaut on space](American%20astronaut%20on%20space.jpg)

via: Pexels / Pixabay

#### 6\. What’s your best childhood memory?

Tapping into childhood memories is a great conversation starter.

#### 7\. What’s your pet peeve?

People can be annoying, but venting helps.

#### 8\. What personality trait has gotten you in the most trouble?

Everyone has a natural tendency that has caused them trouble in the past.

#### 9\. What is the best piece of advice you’ve received?

Good advice works best when it’s shared.

#### 10\. If you had to pick a new name for yourself, what name would you pick?

Some names seem to fit certain personalities.

![Scrabble letters on a table](Scrabble%20letters%20on%20a%20table.jpg)

via: Unsplash / Moritz Schmidt

## 10 Best Fun Questions to Ask

![Three friends sitting on a bench talking](Three%20friends%20sitting%20on%20a%20bench%20talking.jpg)

via: Pixabay / selassie

What are some fun questions to ask? Well, the best fun questions to ask all have a few things in common: they focus on positive ideas and memories, they are great segues for fun stories, and they [show your interest in the person](https://www.entrepreneur.com/article/252070) you’re speaking to.

Use these questions next time you’re with someone riding in the car, sitting down to lunch, or grabbing drinks.

#### 11\. What accomplishment are you most proud of?

Everyone loves a chance to brag a little bit.

#### 12\. Which of your friends are you proudest of? Why?

A person who can recognize greatness in others is the type of person you want to keep around.

#### 13\. What’s the most beautiful place you’ve ever been?

This is a great way to begin a conversation about travel.

#### 14\. Do you feel like a leader or a follower?

All societies needs both types.

#### 15\. What’s the most courageous thing you’ve ever done?

Moments of true courage are rare and inspiring.

#### 16\. When you’re having a bad day, what do you do to make yourself feel better?

Everyone needs a feel-good ritual.

#### 17\. Which would be harder for you to give up: coffee or alcohol?

Which method of self-medicating is most important?

#### 18\. What is your favorite Wikipedia article?

No matter what your teachers said, Wikipedia is the ultimate collection of mankind’s knowledge.

#### 19\. What’s your favorite cheesy pick-up line?

It might be time to try out that old favorite again.

#### 20\. Can I get you another drink?

Everyone’s favorite question.

## 5 Fun Would You Rather Questions

![Diverse friends finished eating at home](Diverse%20friends%20finished%20eating%20at%20home.jpg)

via: Pixabay / angelladagenhart0

[Would you rather questions](https://www.mantelligence.com/would-you-rather-questions/) are like the multiple choice section of a test. You still have to think about your answer, but you have options to choose from.

The best thing about these questions is that there’s no wrong answer, and the questions are all fun.

#### 21\. Would You Rather burp every time you lean in for a kiss or drool every time you talk?

Either way, good luck in the dating scene.

#### 22\. Would You Rather not brush your hair ever or your teeth?

Some of us already make this choice when we’re running late.

#### 23\. Would You Rather sleep wearing handcuffs or a bike helmet?

I guess the answer depends on what else happens in bed.

![Man riding a bicylcle on sunset](Man%20riding%20a%20bicylcle%20on%20sunset.jpg)

via: Unsplash / Sayan Nath

#### 24\. Would You Rather embarrass yourself at a party or get caught making out with your best friend’s sister?

Either one will seriously impact your social life.

#### 25\. Would You Rather be uncontrollably laughing for the rest of your life or uncontrollably crying?

Laughter requires less clean up but can land you in hot water in the wrong setting.

## 7 Fun Questions to Ask to Get to Know Someone

![Happy friends talking on the street](Happy%20friends%20talking%20on%20the%20street.jpg)

[Getting to know someone](https://www.mantelligence.com/how-to-get-to-know-someone/), to really know them, can be a challenge.

It’s a process that requires time and dedication, but as you know from the close relationships you already have, **g****etting to know people is worth the effort.**

These [get to know you questions](https://www.mantelligence.com/good-questions-to-ask-to-get-to-know-someone/) are a fun, easy way to set off on the path towards friendship. Just because it takes some effort, doesn’t mean it can’t be fun.

#### 53\. What are your 3 favorite movies?

Shared taste in movies is a surprisingly good basis for a friendship.

#### 54\. If you had to live in a different state, what would it be?

#### 57\. Are you spiritual or religious in any way? If so, how?

#### 58\. Do you think of yourself as being naughty or nice?

#### 59\. Are you old-fashioned?

## 9 Fun questions to ask a new friend

![Diverse women talking to each other](Diverse%20women%20talking%20to%20each%20other.jpg)

via: Pexels / Ketut Subiyanto

Asking questions to your new friend shouldn't feel like an interview. You should also have some fun **questions to ask a new friend** to give a more relaxed environment for your friend.

#### 60\. What songs have you completely memorized?

And then get ready for them to sing the whole song.

#### 61\. What movie title best describes your life?

Would it be as dramatic as The Notebook or as crazy as The Hangover?

#### 62\. As the only human left on Earth, what would you do?

There are endless possibilities as to what they can do as the only person on Earth. This is definitely a fun question to talk about.

#### 63\. What is one of your favorite smells?

With the abundance of smells in the world, it's impossible not to choose one.

#### 64\. Ever tried putting a bunch of glue on your palm and then peeling it off?

The real question is... who hasn't?

#### 66\. What's the funniest joke you've ever heard?

#### 67\. Who would you trade places with someone for a day and why?

If you want a fun question, this one should be in your options. Dive into why one person's life is so interesting to live.

#### 68\. Do you love pineapples on pizza?

The never-ending debate.

## 7 Fun Interview Questions

![Group of people in a serious discussion](Group%20of%20people%20in%20a%20serious%20discussion.jpg)

via: Unsplash / You X Ventures

When you're choosing candidates for your team, you'll need some **fun interview questions** to go along with the regular **[interview questions](https://www.mantelligence.com/interview-questions/).**

#### 78\. What's something that's true that almost nobody agrees with you on?

We all have something we take seriously and believe in strongly and it's the rest of the world that needs to wake up to our brilliance.

#### 79\. If you were starting a company tomorrow, what would the top 3 core values for your company be?

How well can the candidate articulate their core values?

#### 80\. How do you feel about sharing your password with your partner?

This would depend on the kind of information that's available behind the passwords and how balanced the partner is and the level of trust the two individuals feel safe cultivating for each other.

#### 81\. Do you judge a book by its cover?

Don't we all? And don't we feel better to know exactly how others do?

![Men talking to each other](Men%20talking%20to%20each%20other.jpg)

via: Unsplash / Product School

#### 82\. If a genie granted you 3 wishes right now, what would you wish for?

Wildest dreams or practicalities? Selfish wishes or Manifestations for the world and ending suffering? This shows how far outside of their own survival your candidate is willing to consider.

#### 83\. What’s something that people think makes them look cool but actually has the opposite effect?

How strong is your candidate's sense of irony and ability to choose authenticity over masking? If someone's trying for cool and it's backfiring there's definitely some masking and insecurity going on. Can the candidate sniff those things out?

#### 84\. How does someone politely end a conversation with a person who won't stop talking?

Tact is one of the most valuable talents out there.

## 10 Fun Ice Breaker Questions

![Two beautiful women smiling at camera](Two%20beautiful%20women%20smiling%20at%20camera.jpg)

via: Unsplash / Thought Catalog

Get everyone giggling with these witty **[ice breaker questions](https://www.mantelligence.com/ice-breaker-questions/).** Get everyone feeling friendly with some next-level [**ice breaker games.**](https://www.mantelligence.com/ice-breaker-games/)

#### 85\. What wastes the most time in your day to day life?

A person's boundaries around time-wasting say a lot about their emotional burdens and self-discipline. On the other side, what's considered a waste of time by one person is not for another. And some people are really hard on themselves and consider self-care a waste of time because they struggle to value themselves.

#### 86\. Why do they call them "Free Gifts"? Aren't all gifts free?

There's an oxymoron to wade through like quicksand and taffy. Hopefully, the answerer enjoys this sort of quandary.

![Woman holding paper bags](Woman%20holding%20paper%20bags.jpg)

via: Pexels / Porapak Apichodilok

#### 87\. If a criminal turns himself in, shouldn't he get the reward money?

This is a gauge for compassion. Many criminals aren't inherently bad. Wouldn't everyone who can't get employment fast enough or due to discrimination be driven to steal in order to provide for their child whom they love?

#### 88\. Why does a round pizza come in a square box?

If you've seen the octagonal shaped boxes that some pizzas come in, you'll notice they're more complicated to construct than a square box...

#### 89\. Do they bury people with their braces on?

This brings up how most people braces aren't of typical age for dying which makes this question kind of sad actually.

#### 90\. Why is the man who invests your money called a broker?

What exactly has broken here?

#### 91\. Why are dogs noses always wet?

Do they come up with something witty or do they go the nerdy science route?

![Dog lying on the floor](Dog%20lying%20on%20the%20floor.jpg)

via: Pexels / Bruno Cervera

#### 92\. Can you cry underwater?

Has a tree still fallen if nobody hears it crash?

#### 93\. How old are you before it can be said you died of old age?

Is old age a cop-out? Does it just mean there was no ravaging disease present?

#### 94\. If I had my legs amputated, would I have to change my height and weight on my driver's license?

With prosthetics available, this is an interesting question.

You know how important it is to make a good first impression, and you now you know dozens of fun questions to ask in order to ensure that you’re remembered positively.

Even though it’s hard to go wrong with these questions I have a few tips to help you get the most out of them.

### 1\. Adapt

![a notepad](a%20notepad.jpg)

via: Pexels / Polina Kovaleva

Even though this list has questions for every scenario, not every question is best for every setting. **Choose the questions that make sense for the context you’re in**.

### 2\. Listen

![Two men listening to a woman](Two%20men%20listening%20to%20a%20woman.jpg)

via: Unsplash / Priscilla Du Preez

If you want [people to open up about themselves](https://www.psychologytoday.com/blog/stronger-the-broken-places/201409/12-steps-getting-someone-open), you’ll have to **show them that you’re listening**. Ask follow up questions and show interest in the person you’re speaking with.

### 3\. Be open

![People outside a restaurant](People%20outside%20a%20restaurant.jpg)

via: Unsplash / Henrique Félix

**Fun conversations depend on equal participation**. Be prepared to answer your own questions and to share your stories when asked.
