# Fireside talks
- How to develop key competency in your own ways?
	- Be a sponge. Absorb a lot of learnings from other people. 
	- Network. Be bold to reach out to other people that you don't know.
		- When reaching out, know what your purpose and think of how you can give value back.
	- Do not box yourself in a specific knowledge/skill.
	- Be careful of the path that you want to take after school. 
	- Question the status quo. Question things. If you have a brilliant idea in mind, and there is resistant to it, you have to be pushy about it. - Angelica Ferrer
- What is an impressive trait - that could give us a good reputation as a fresh grad?
	- For fresh grads, character pays off more than the skills.
		- Be honest. 
			- Even though you have nothing to offer. (Some companies are looking for people who are willing to learn.)
			- Talk about your challenges and how you really worked on it.
			- Character is more important than skill.
		- Be authentic and open to new learnings.
		- Be hungry for learnings. 
- What are skills that are significant in ensuring a good relationships with your peers?
	- Being able to build a personal relationship with your workmates.
	- Have empathy. 
		- See your other people as human beings. 
		- Be genuine and have genuine interest to get to know them.
