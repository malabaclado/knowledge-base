The foraging behaviour of eagles such as golden eagles or Aquila Chrysaetos is inspiring. An eagle forages in its own territory by flying freely in a random manner much like the Levy flights. Once the prey is sighted, the eagle will change its search strategy to an intensive chasing tactics so as to catch the prey as efficiently as possible.

1. Random search in the whole domain following the Levy distribution. 
2. Intensive chasing strategy following the [[Firefly algorithm|firefly algorithm]].
