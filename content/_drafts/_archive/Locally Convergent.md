# Locally Convergent
#definition #course/math-171 #topic/math/numerical-analysis 


---
==**Definition.**== An iterative method is called **locally convergent to r** if the method converges to r for *any *initial guesses sufficiently close to r.


==**Intuition:**== A method is locally convergent to $r$ if there exists a neighborhood $(r-\epsilon,r+\epsilon)$ such that convenience to $r$ follows from all initial guesses from that neighborhood.
