
#### Hero Types

##### Tank

- High HP
- High base defense rating.

##### Assassin
General Characteristics: 
- Burst damage
- Low HP



##### Mage
General Characteristics: 
- Unique skill effects
- Low basic attack damage


##### Marksman
- Unique basic attack effects
- Long-range capability


##### Support
- Unique support skills that can strengthen other heroes.
- Lower HP than Tanks
- Lower push capability thahn Fighters
- Lower damage than Mages
