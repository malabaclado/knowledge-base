# 2023-02-11
- This reddit post contains tips on [how to avoid midday energy crashes](https://www.reddit.com/r/productivity/comments/10ysle9/mid_day_energy_crash_advice/)
	- Try delaying coffee by 90-120 mins after waking
		- Why?  When we wake up, there is a natural, healthy cortisol increase which will wash away adenosine which makes us tired. Drinking caffeine will interfere with that process, keeping the adenosine around which causes a crash in the late afternoon.
