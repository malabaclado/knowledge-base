#### Course Outline
1.  Introduction to MATLAB and LaTeX

	   a. Machine representation of numbers
	   b. Floating point arithmetic
	   c. Sources of errors
	   d. Error propagation and generation
	   e. Accuracy, convergence, and Big-oh notation

4. [[ Solution of Nonlinear Equations]]
	a. Review of analysis
	b. Bisection algorithm
	c. Fixed point iteration method
	d. Newton-Raphson iteration method
	e. Error analysis of iterative methods
5.  [[Interpolation]]
    a. Polynomial interpolation
    b. Cubic spline interpolation
6.  Numerical Integration and Differentiation
    a. Trapezoidal Rule
    b. Simpson’s Rule
    c. Newton-Cotes Formulas
    d. Finite differences
7.  Numerical Solution of Initial Value Problems of Ordinary Differential Equations
    a. Euler’s method
    b. Runge-Kutta methods
8.  Solution of Linear Systems
    a. Gauss-Jordan Elimination
    b. Lower-upper triangular factorization of matrices
    c. Iterative methods for solving linear systems
    d. Error analysis of iterative methods
9.  Numerical Solution of Boundary Value Problems of Ordinary Differential Equations
    a. Finite difference method for Boundary Value Problem
