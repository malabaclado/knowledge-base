# Review of machine learning methods for insurance pricing and fraud detection

- **Insurance pricing** 
	- [@hanafy2021](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-9091/9/2/42)  considers how automotive insurance providers incorporate machinery learning in their company, and explores how ML models can apply to insurance big data. ==They used  various ML methods, such as logistic regression, XGBoost, random forest, decision trees, naïve Bayes, and K-NN, to predict claim occurrence.== The results showed that RF is better than other methods.
	- [@chapados2021](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2001/hash/2d405b367158e3f12d7c1e31a96b3af3-Abstract.html)  found that function approximation methods that do not optimize a squared loss, like Support Vector Machines regression, do not work well in estimating insurance premium with the large number of variables, many of which are discrete, and the very peculiar shape of the noise distribution, asymmetric with fat tails, with a large majority zeros and a few unreliable and very large values
	- [@spedicato2021](https://hal.archives-ouvertes.fr/hal-01942038/) explored the applicability of novel machine learning techniques such as ==tree boosted models to optimise the proposed premium on prospective policyholders==. Given their predictive gain over GLMs, we carefully analyse both the advantages and disadvatanges induced by their use
- **Fraud detection** 
	- [@roy2020](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/8074258) focused on detecting the auto\vehicle fraud by using, machine learning technique. The performance will be compared by calculation of confusion matrix which help to calculate accuracy, precision, and recall.
	- [@severino2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666827021000372)  evaluated fraud prediction in property insurance claims using various [machine learning](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/computer-science/machine-learning "Learn more about machine learning from ScienceDirect's AI-generated Topic Pages") models based on real-world data from a major Brazilian insurance company. ==The results showed that ensemble-based methods (random forest and gradient boosting) and deep neural networks yielded the best results, exhibiting superior average performance in comparison to the other classifiers, including the commonly used logistic regression.==
	- [@kose2015](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1568494615004585) implemented and evaluated a novel framework to detect fraudulent and abusive cases independently from the actors and commodities involved in the claims and an extensible structure to introduce new fraud and abuse types.
- **Review/Others**
	- [@blier-wong](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-9091/9/1/4) reviewed the applications of machine learning to the actuarial science field and presented the current state of the art in ratemaking and reserving.
	- [@qazi2020](https://wires.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/widm.1363) used Bayesian Networks for modeling our systems. They also presented a deep-learning-based approach to provide recommendations to prospects (potential customers) where only external marketing data is available at the time of prediction.
