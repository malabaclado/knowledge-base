# Getting Hired with a Standout Resume
Speaker: Jheric Delos Angeles

- [ ] Check out: BUKAS.PH

Your resume is a storytelling document.

Tips for fresh graduates:
- Fresh grad resume must-haves
	- Extra-curricular activities
		- Highlight the projects you're worked on
	- Internship experience
	- Skills
		- Highlight your best skills
		- Prioritize skills that are not common (what makes you unique?) Wag puro MS skills
- Questions to ask yourself: 
	- What can you do for the company?
	- Are the experiences you add relevant to your role?
- Hiring managers look for the impact you created in your past work experiences. 
	- What projects did you do?
	- What is the impact of your work to the company?
- Tip: Get keywords from the Job Description. Customize your resume to a job description.

# 5 questions to ask yourself when crafting a killer resume
Speaker: Myke Celis
What I want to be: Machine Learning Engineer/ Data Scientist

1. What is my resume for?
	- Anong industry ba inaapplyan mo?
		- First line: Focus on your strength (eg. Aspiring leader looking for...)
2. What can show my personal brand clearly?
	- Your brand:
		- Your image
		- Your mission
		- Your values
		- Your vision
3. What do I need to highlight?
	1. Strong and clear sumary *(Never put 'land in any position')*
	2. Success stories
	3. Relevant skills
4. Who can vouch for me?
5. What do I need to professionalize more?
	- Check your: Picture, Email address, Social media pages
	- Picture should give your first impression
		- For a tech guy: something clean, intelligent (with glasses), and approachable (bright & crisp)
