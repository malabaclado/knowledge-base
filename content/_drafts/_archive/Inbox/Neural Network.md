# Artificial Neural Network
Artificial Neural Networks are computing systems inspired by the biological neural networks that constitute animal brains. These artificial networks may be used for predictive modeling, adaptive control and applications where they can be trained via a dataset. 


One advantage of a neural network is that you can gain insights as to how the process really work.
