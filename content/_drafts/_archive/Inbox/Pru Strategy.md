# How to do it *(Strategies)*
- ==Streamline business processes==
	- Have a consistent CRM
	- Build desired number of people in your CRM (and ways to replenish it)
	- Set a number of people to approach everyday
	- Create quick price quotations table based on age (later, it could be based on occupation such as teachers, bpo, seafarers, etc)
	- Prepare presentations
		- 5-mins pitch
		- Life stage financial planning
		- What is insurance and why you need one?
		- What is VUL?
		- What is UITF?
	- Prepare one page proposal template
	- Business Card
- ==Develop strong branding==
	- Own feed
		- Types of post: Entertain, Educate, Engage
		- Reels
			- Make reels about life insurance and needs. Post 2x per month.
		- Feed
			- *Tip: Always post something interesting.*
			- Post weekly on Facebook profile weekly.
				- About being a financial advisor
				- About benefits of being insured
				- Post photo of yourself with quotes
		- Stories:
			- Post stories with actual "stories". Be madaldal!
			- Good stories niche:
				- **Places.** Places I go to + some interestin story about it
				- **Life with PRU.** Journey with PRU (What are you currently working on??) + Show that you are genuinely enjoying the mission of securing people's lives.
				- **Finance.** Finance tips
					- Banks VS VUL comparison
					- Finance tips from reputable people (tiktok, twitter, insta)
				- People I hang out with + interesting story about them
				- ==Client feedbacks==
	- Network with Facebook Groups
		- Become active in Philippine Insurance Forum
		- Become active in Financial Literacy PH
- ==Strengthen relationships==
	- Reconnect with people you don't usually talk to (No insurance talks)
	- Strengthen relationships with your current clients (support them, make yourself seen by them)
	- Shoot your shot!
