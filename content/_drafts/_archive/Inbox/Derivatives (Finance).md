# Derivatives (Finance)

In finance, **derivatives** are securities whose payoffs are based on the value of other assets, so-called underlying assets, for example, stocks, currencies, bonds, commodities, etc.

Four popular types of derivatives are futures, options, forwards, and swaps.

> Financial derivatives play an important role in the financial markets because they are used to hedge risks occurring from the operational, financing and investment activities of companies (Lehmann, 2017). [[@obthongSurveyMachineLearning2020]]
