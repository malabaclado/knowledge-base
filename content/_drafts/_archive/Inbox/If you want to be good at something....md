**If you want to become an amazing financial advisor, you have to connect with your stakeholders**

**You have to network with people who needs financial advice such as income earners and business owners.**

**You also have to be amazing in your own right. You have to display your expertise - and that you have to keep them conected.**

**Question is: How do you want people to see you?**
- As an active agent who works at Pru Life UK.
- As someone who has lots of interests but is down-to-earth approachable.
- As someone who is in the process of learning about finance.

As Aristotle says, *you are what you repeatedly do.*

**What can you do to build your desire image as a financial advisor?**
1. Post trainings, meetings and images showing you are affiliated with Pru Life UK.
2. Post your interests:
	1. Reading books (self-help, finance)
	2. Working out
	3. Socializing with people
	4. Travelling to places
	5. Interesting stuff but should be in moderation: mobile legends, movies, memes
3. Post insights and tips about finance.
4. Share valuable stuff


**Main principles from this reflection**
If you want to be good at something:
1. Connect with people who do and are interested with what you do.
2. Share your activities, insights and realizations.
3. Share stuff that is of value.
