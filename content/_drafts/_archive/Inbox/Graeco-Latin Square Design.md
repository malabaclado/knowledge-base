Graeco-Latin is simply two superimposed [[Latin Square Design]] with one using the Latin Letters and the other using the Greek letters, resulting in a design with four factors

Therefore, the error can be controlled in three directions by blocking three nuisance factors. Two treatment factors could also be tested by blocking only two nuisance factors.
