# Eagle search algorithm
The [[Eagle search algorithm]] is a nature-inspired technique for solving optimisation problems that mimics the behaviour of blad eagles when searching for food. 

Bald eagles search for food in three stages. (1) The eagles identify an area where they will conduct their search, (2) The eagles search for food within the selected area and (3) The eagles choose and attack a prey.
