# Projective Geometry

[[Projective Geometry]] deals with the relationship between the geometric figures and their images that result from projecting the original objects to other surfaces. ^95134e

![[Pasted image 20220223204458.png]] ^00902a

Example: Shadow of opaque objects, motion pictures 

[[Projective Geometry]] can be regarded as an extended Euclidean geometry where distance and angles are not given much thought and ==where parallel lines can meet== at what we call an [[Ideal Point]]. ^decb99

[[Projective Geometry]] has its origins from the Italian Renaissance, primarily conceived in the context of art. 

- Desargues - known as the Father of Projective Geometry
- Pascal - pioneered the study of preserved *invariant* properties
- Poncelet - coined the concept of *duality*.
