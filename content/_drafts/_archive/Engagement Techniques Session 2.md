- [Filipinos spend an ave of 4.15 hours on social media everyday.](https://i.imgur.com/8RNkAUW.png)
- [Goal for social media usage](https://i.imgur.com/u7dZyvX.png): 
	- Be remembered (Awareness)
	- Be liked (Approachability)
	- Be trusted (Trust building)
- Is chatting enough for online engagement?
	- No
- Ideal creation:
	- 20% Expertise
	- 20% Skill
	- 60% Trust
- # Opening Statements
	- Start with a greeting and address them by their name. 
		- Para hindi mukhang copy-pasted
		- ![](https://i.imgur.com/6BLYyHh.png)
	- Use stickers, emojis and memes effectively and smartly.
- # Four steps of opening statements and chatting
	- Break the ice - short, simple opening statement
		- Greetings
		- Quick check up
	- Warm-up
	- Spark interest - why are you chatting the person in the first place? 
		- Be their friend first.
		- ![|400](https://i.imgur.com/vJIOQe7.png)

	- Conclude
- Add value in 4 ways:
	- Knowledge
	- Experience
	- Network
	- Time
- Keep things sweet and simple.
- Tips
	- NO to asking questions to Level D prospects
	- Start with a greeeting mentioning their name
	- Use of relevant images is a game-changer.
	- Dapat magaan sa feeling ka kausap. As a friend talaga
