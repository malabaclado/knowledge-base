**Early Computers**
- The earliest computing device in history is the *Abacus* which was used during the 2500 BCE in Mesopotamia region.

- From there, different tools that aid in computations were made such as - astrolabe (for navigation).

- The earliest documented use of the word 'computer' is in the year 1613 on the book by Richard Braithwith.
	- In his book, the word 'computer' refer to a person whose job is to compute.

- In 1694, mathematician Gottfried Leibniz created the machine called Step Reckoner.
	- Step Reckoner is the first machine to ever compute all four basic mathematical operations.

**Charles Babbage and the Difference Engine**
- In 1824, Charles Babbage attempted to create what he calls the Difference Engine.
- The creation of the Difference Engine was abandoned at around 1843.
- In 1991, historians successfully created the Difference Engine from Babbage's notes.

**The Analytical Engine**
- The Analytical Engine was a hypothetical machine imagined by Charles Babbage.
- Ada Lovelace wrote hypothetical programs for this machine. Because of this, Ada Lovelace is considered as the world's first programmer.
- The analytical engine would later be the inspiration for the creation of computers that we know today.
- Charles Babbage was considered the 'Father of Computing'

**The Tabulating Machine**
- The US Census problem: Around 1890, the US constitution demand that they keep census every 10 years. But the estimated time it took to complete the census are too long.
- By the end of the 19th century, Herman Hollerith created the tabulating machine to solve the US Census problem.
- He also founded the Tabulating Machine Company. This company later evolved to what we now know as the International Business Machines (IBM) Corporation.
