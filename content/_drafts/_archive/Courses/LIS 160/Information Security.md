# Threats and Mitigation Strategies
## Hardware Loss or Failure

## Unauthorized access and use
- Put passwords on your devices.
- Use multi-factor authentication	
	- Two factor: Something you know, something you have
		- eg. phone and password, debit card and password
	- Three-factor: Something you know, something you have, something you are
		- eg. face recognition and fingerprint
	- Four-factor: Something you know, something you have, something you are, your location
