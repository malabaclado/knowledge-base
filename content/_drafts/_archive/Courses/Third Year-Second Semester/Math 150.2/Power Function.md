> Definition. (**Power Function**)
> Let $C$ be the critical region of the null hypothesis $H_0$. The power function of C, denoted by $\Pi_C(\theta)$, is defined as the probabiltiy that $H_0$ is rejected. That is, 
> $$\begin{align}
> \Pi_C(\theta) &=\mathbb{P}[reject H_0]\\
> &= \mathbb{P} [(X_1, X_2,...,X_n) \in C]
> \end{align}$$
