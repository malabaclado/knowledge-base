> **Definition. (Uniformly Most Powerful Test)**
> A test $\Upsilon^**$ where $H_0=\theta \in \Omega_0$ against $H_a:\theta \in \Omega - \Omega_0$ is a **uniformly most powerful test (UMPT) of size $\alpha$** if and only if the following conditions are satisfied:
> 1. $\sup_{\theta \in \Omega_0} \Pi_{\Upsilon^*}(\theta)=\alpha$
> 2. $\Pi_{\Upsilon^*}(\theta) \geq \Pi_{\Upsilon}(\theta)$ for every $\theta \in \Omega - \Omega_0$ and for any test $\Upsilon$ with size less than or equal to $\alpha$.




#### Remarks
- UPPT does not exist for all testing problems. It may or may not exist.
