##### Course Outline
Sampling Distributions 
1. Random Sample 
2. Statistics and Sampling Distributions 
3. Distributions Derived from the Normal Distribution
4. Order statistics 
5. Limit Theorems (Law of Large Numbers, Central Limit Theorem)


Point Estimation 
1. Introduction and Definitions for Parametric Point Estimation 
2. Optimal Properties of Point Estimators 
3. Methods of Finding Point Estimators

Interval Estimation 
1. Introduction and Definitions for Parametric Confidence Interval Estimation 
2. Optimal Properties of Confidence Interval Estimators 
3. Methods of Finding Confidence Interval Estimators
4. Sampling from the Normal Distribution

Hypothesis Testing 
1. [[Introduction and Definitions for Parametric Hypothesis Testing ]]
2. Optimal Properties of Tests of Hypothesis (Power of a Test, Unbiasedness)
3. Methods of Constructing Tests of Hypothesis 
4. Sampling from the Normal Distribution (One and Two Sample Case Test on Paired Observations) 
5. Relationship Between Hypothesis Testing and Confidence Interval

Applications 
1. Introduction to Statistical Data Analysis (Sampling and Experimental Designs, Levels of Measurement, Normality Assumption, Choosing the Appropriate Statistical Method, Statistical vs Practical Significance) 
2. Tests for Means 
3. Tests for Variances 
4. Tests for Proportions (Binomial test) 
5. Measures of Association (Correlation and Simple Linear Regression) 
6. Tests for Equality of Means on Multiple Population (Analysis of Variance) 
7. Tests of Independence (Chi-square Tests and Contingency Tables) 
8. Goodness-of-Fit Tests 
9. Nonparametric Statistic
