> Definition. (**Significance Level**)
> Let C be a critical region of the null hypothesis $H_0$.
> The size of the critical region $\alpha$ is: $$\alpha = \sup [\Pi_C(\theta)] $$
> We call $\alpha$ the significance level of the test.
