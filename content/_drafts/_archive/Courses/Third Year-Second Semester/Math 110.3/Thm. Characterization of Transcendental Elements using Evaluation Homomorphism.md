This theorem gives a characterization of transcendental elements using the evaluation homomorphism.

> Theorem. (Characterization of Transcendental Elements using Evaluation Homomorphism)
> Let E be an extension field of a field F and let $\alpha \in E$. Let $\phi_{\alpha}:F[x] \to E$ be the evaluation homomorphism of $F[x]$ into $E$ such that $\phi_{\alpha}(a)=a$ and $\phi_{\alpha}(x)=\alpha$. Then, $\alpha$ is transcendental over F if and only if $\phi_{\alpha}$ is a one-to-one map.

#### Proof of Theorem
We'll prove this theorem using direct proof.

$$\begin{align}
\alpha \text{ is transcendental} &\Longleftrightarrow \text{For all nonzero polynomial } f(x) \in F[x], \space f(\alpha)\neq 0 \\
&\Longleftrightarrow	\ker \phi_{\alpha}= \{0\}\\
&\Longleftrightarrow \phi_{\alpha} \text{ is one-to-one}
\end{align}$$

*End of proof.*
