> Definition. (**Algebraic and Transcendental Elements**)
> Let E be an extension field of a field F and $\alpha$ be an element of E. We say $\alpha$ is **algebraic over F** if $f(\alpha)=0$ for some nonzero polynomial $f(x) \in F[x]$. Otherwise, $\alpha$ is **transcendental over F**.

---
#### Remarks

We say an element $\alpha$ is algebraic over a field $F$ if it is a zero of some polynomial $f(x)$ over $F$.

---

#### Examples
1. All elements of a field $\alpha \in F$ is algebraic over $F$ 
2. The element $i \in \mathbb{C}$ is algebraic over $\mathbb{Q}$
3. $\sqrt{2}$ is algebraic over $\mathbb{Q}$
4. $e$, $\pi$, $\sin 1$ are transcendental over $\mathbb{Q}$
	- But $e$ and $\pi$ are algebraic over $\mathbb{R}$ since $f(x)=x-e$ and $g(x)=x-\pi$ are polynomials in $\mathbb{R}$
