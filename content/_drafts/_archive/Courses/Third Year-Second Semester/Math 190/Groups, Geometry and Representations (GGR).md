**Presentor**: Jade Ventura
**Research Interest**:  Differential Geometry, General Relativity, Mathematical Physics
**Presentation Title:** Modelling Gravity (and other forces of nature) and Model Deformation (PHD Research)

**Used Concepts**: 
- There are 4 fundamental forces of nature
- Gravitational force: space-time models
- For the 3 other forces: quantum mechanical models

He explained how to connect math models to physics models.

Thoughts On Modelling
1. Working within mathematical theory. 
2. Consider the *field* of application. (The physics that you want to model.)
3. Declare a rule between the math theory and the field.
4. Use the rule (theorems) to explain the field using math. Explain or predict physical phenomenon using math theorems.
