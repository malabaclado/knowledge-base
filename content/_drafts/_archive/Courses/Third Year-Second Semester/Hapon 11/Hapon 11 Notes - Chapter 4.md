##  How to use the verbs います and あります.
います and あります are both used to say that 'something' exists. The choice of word depends on whether the object is living or non-living. Both verbs use the particle: が.
<!--ID: 1645617151813-->


-   あります - used for ==non-living things and plants== (non-moving)
    -   Dictionary form: ある
-   います - used for ==living things==
    - Dictionary form: いる
<!--ID: 1619312065885-->

---

**Function 1: Telling that something exists**

>   There is a tree over there.
>   あそこにきがあります。

Remark: が - marks the object of the verb (exists → tree)

---

**Function 2: Telling the location of something**

>   There is an enemy over there.
>   あそこにてきがいます

てき means enemy/opponent.

>   What is in Vertis North?
>   Vertis North になにがありますか。
    
>   There are restaurants in Vertis North.
>   Vertis North にレストランがあります。
    
>   What's in your town?
>   あなたのまちになにがありますか。
    
>   There's a park in my town.
>   わたしのまちにこうえんがあります。
    
>   Do you have a pet in your house?
>   いえにペットがいますか。
>   - Quick Answer: はい、います。/ いいえ、ペットがいません。

---

**Function 3: Asking the existence of something or someone.**

Format: noun + が + (あります/ います) + か。

>   Do you have **siblings**?
>   **きょうだい**がいますか。
    
>   Do you have **free time**?
>   **じかん**がありますか。
    
>   I have something to talk about./ I have a story to tell.
>   はなしがあります。
    
>   I have a **question**.
>   **しつもん**があります。
    

---
**Function 4: Looking for something/someone.**

>   Where is my **money**?
>   おかねはどこにありますか。
>    - Answer 1: おかねはあそこです。(There's your money; This is used if there's a shared vision. You and the person can see the money.)
>    - Answer 2: おかねはGenshinにあります。(Your money is (spent) in Genshin.

>   Where is my brother?
>   おにいさんはどこでいますか。
> -   If brother can be seen around:
> 	-   あそこです。
> 	-   _Your brother is in there._
> -   If brother cannot be seen. (Example, in Japan)
> 	-   おにいさんはにほんにいます。
> 	-   _Your brother is in Japan_

>   Where are you right now?
>   いま、どこにいますか。
>   -   Answer: わたしは\*\[Your location\]\*にいます。

## Uses of the particle:も
- も replaces the particles は、が and を to mark an object in common characteristics as that of the subject.
- Particles other than は、が and を are used together with も rather than being replaced by it.
- You may use も when you go to two places, on two several occasions, and so forth.
<!--ID: 1619312066117-->


> I went to Kyoto last week. I went to Osaka too.
> 私はせんしゅうきょとにいきました。おおさか==にも==いきました。

## Expression of time: ～じかん
- A number with suffix ~じかん expresses a time duration. Such a noun is not followed by any article.
- For estimation of duration, add the word ぐらい after the ~じかん.
- To say one hour and a half., add the word はん immediately after じかん.
<!--ID: 1619312066166-->


---
## Expression of quantity: たくさん
- たくさん　means "in large quantity"
- You can add it to a direct object by either: placing it before a noun or after the particle を.
<!--ID: 1619312066205-->


> I took many pictures.
> わたしはしゃしんをたくさんとりました。
> わたしはたくさんしゃしんをとりました。

---
## Uses of the Particle: と
The particle と has two functions:
1. Connect two nouns A and B. 
<!--ID: 1619312066245-->


> Meat and vegetables
> にくとやさい

2. Describes *with whom* you do something.

> Mary will go to Korea with Sue
> メアリーさんはスーさんとかんこくにいきます。
