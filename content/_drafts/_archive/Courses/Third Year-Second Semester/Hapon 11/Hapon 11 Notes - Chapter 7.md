##### Durative and Instantaneous Verbs
Here, we introduce two new classification of verbs according to the duration in which they apply.
<!--ID: 1645617151797-->


1. Durative - continuous duration of time
2. Instantaneous - like on and off kind of thing (binary thing); stative 

---

##### Uses of ~ています
The **～ています form** of a verb generally means a **currently taken action**. This is somewhat similar (with some nuances) to the *progressive form* of verbs (aka *-ing form*) in English language. In 日本語, the nuances depends on whether the verb can be categorized as durative or instantaneous.
<!--ID: 1645617151804-->


**For durative verbs: **

1. **currently doing** (as in with duration); **always preceded by いま**

	*Example:* いま、たべています。(currently eating)

2. **current undertaking** (can be intermittent)

	*Example:* れんしゅうをしていませ　(I exercise)
	Note that the speaker may not be exercising at the moment, it just mean that *they exercise* recently or intermittently (as in a regular basis).

**For instantaneous verbs:**

3. **current, long running state**

	*Example:* けっこんしています (currently married)
	Note that the speaker does not mean they are currently at the event and getting married. It means that their current state is "married".

4. **current observable state**

	*Example: *まどのちかくにすわっています (sitting near the window)
	Note: Unlike in the previous example, here the speaker directly observes the subject.


---
**Example: Asking what people do：**
なにをしていますか。 (What do you do?/ What do you do in life?)
いま、なにをしていますか。(What are you doing right now?)

**ます vs ています**

ます vs ています vs ました	


**Durative**
- うたう to sing


**Instantaneous**
- しる to know
- (まちに)すむ to live(in a place)
- (Tshirt)をきる to put on (upper body clothing); conjugates to ～をきて
- ~をはく to put on (lower body clothing); conjugates to ～をはいて
- ～をかぶる to put on (headwear); conjugates to かぶって
- ～をする to use accessories; conjugates to して
- (めがねを)かける wear an eyeglass; conjugates to めがねをかけて
- ふとる to gain weight/ to be heavy; conjugates to ふとって
- やせる to lose weight; conjugates to やせて
- (かいしゃに)つとめる - to work for (a company); conjugates to つとめて
- (だれかと)けっこん


**Some examples：**
くろのTシャツをきています (I wear a black tshirt)
ショーツをはいています。(I wear shorts)

めがねかけていますか。(Do you wear glasses?)
はい、かけています。(Yes, I wear glasses)
いいえ、かけていません (No, I don't)

どこにすんでいますか。(Where do you live?)
Cavite にすんでいます。(I live in Cavite)

~すんでいました (used to live)
Manilaにすんでいました (I used to live in Manila)
私もUP Village にすんでいました (I used to live in UP Village too)

**すっしん vs すんでいます**
すっしん - where you come from/ grew up
すんでいます - where you currently reside

**ふとる and やせる**
ふとりました -> gained weight
ふとっています　-> fat (current state)

The cat is fat.
ねこはふとっています。

**Another use of ています**　: Describing characteristics

**Describing people's clothing**
**Describing appearances (body part)**
- Format: (subject)　は　(body part) が　(adjective)

**Describing using two adjectives**
- Use て-form for adjectives
- For な-adjectives, the te-conjugation is formed by simply adding で
- For い-adjectives, remove the last い and replace it by くて.
