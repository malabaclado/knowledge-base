Source: [Master The Perfect Push Up | How to Do a Push Up Correctly - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vt-IbHbN7S8)

1. Lie straight on the ground, keep feet together.
2. Arms outside the chest, fingers out wide for maximum grip.
3. Engage your core, tighten up the glutes.
4. Push up then move the chest down to the ground.
5. Avoid srugging and overarching your back.
