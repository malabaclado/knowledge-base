**What is your purpose for interpolating and extrapolating data?**

**Interpolation** is a type of estimation, a method of constructing new data points based on the range of a discrete set of known data points. Used to make basic inferences on how your data behaves.
 

 **Extrapolation** is a type of estimation, beyond the original observation range, of the value of a variable on the basis of its relationship with another variable. It is similar to interpolation, which produces estimates between known observations, but extrapolation is subject to greater uncertainty and a higher risk of producing meaningless results.
 
**TLDR:** Interpolation estimates within the data range. Extrapolation estimates beyond the data range, which is subject to greater uncertainty.
