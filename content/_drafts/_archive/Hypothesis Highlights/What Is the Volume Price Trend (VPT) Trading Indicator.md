## Highlights
- The volume price trend (VPT) indicator helps determine a security’s price direction and strength of price change. The indicator consists of a cumulative volume line that adds or subtracts a multiple of the percentage change in a share price’s trend and current volume, depending upon the security’s upward or downward movements. — [Updated on 2023-04-06 12:08:50](https://hyp.is/vFvpWtQwEe2kWI-5XOxHRQ/www.investopedia.com/terms/v/vptindicator.asp) — Group: #Just-Me

- The volume price trend indicator is used to determine the balance between a security’s demand and supply. The percentage change in the share price trend shows the relative supply or demand of a particular security, while volume indicates the force behind the trend. — [Updated on 2023-04-06 12:09:12](https://hyp.is/ycUtBNQwEe2cRwv6BK1PRA/www.investopedia.com/terms/v/vptindicator.asp) — Group: #Just-Me

- The VPT indicator is similar to the on-balance volume (OBV) indicator in that it measures cumulative volume and provides traders with information about a security’s money flow. Most charting software packages have the VPT indicator included. — [Updated on 2023-04-06 12:09:27](https://hyp.is/0pDjJNQwEe2SGstH_ZLXfw/www.investopedia.com/terms/v/vptindicator.asp) — Group: #Just-Me
