- A predetermined number of reps are assigned for each set, and they must be completed in a minute. If completed faster, a longer rest break may be taken. If the exerciser moves slowly, however, he or she will have a limited rest break before the next set. — [Updated on 2021-12-29 14:23:27](https://hyp.is/1WmMFGhvEeysqCdbU3SzKg/www.acefitness.org/education-and-resources/lifestyle/blog/6535/emom-workout/) — Group: #Just-Me

- If the goal of the EMOM workout is endurance and a metabolic challenge (as demonstrated in the workout below), select a number of reps and resistance for each exercise that should take just under one minute, allowing for very short rest breaks. If strength is the goal, select only a few heavy reps for each set, and the rest break will be built right in. You could also set a higher time limit and choose a series of exercises to fit into that time and repeated for several rounds. Here’s a sample workout: 5 box jumps, 10 dumbbell rows, 10 push-ups, and 20 skater leaps in three minutes for 10 rounds. — [Updated on 2021-12-29 14:25:04](https://hyp.is/Dyi28GhwEeysq9MnSGHLoQ/www.acefitness.org/education-and-resources/lifestyle/blog/6535/emom-workout/) — Group: #Just-Me
