# Personal Equity and Retirement Account (PERA)
PERA is short for Personal Equity and Retirement Account created under Republic Act No. 9505. 

It is a voluntary pension fund that is regulated by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP). It lets you invest in various types of managed funds, which would help you prepare for retirement. By the time you reach the age of 55 and have invested in a PERA fund for at least 5 years, you’ll be able to redeem your PERA investments tax-free.

- By the time you reach the age of 55 and have invested in a PERA fund for at least 5 years, you’ll be able to redeem your PERA investments tax-free.
- Aside from the aforementioned tax-free redemptions for PERA investments, you’ll be able to enjoy a 5% tax credit as long as the annual PERA Contribution does not exceed Php 100,000 for Filipinos based in the Philippines and Php 200,000 for OFWs.
- Another benefit of PERA is that in case of death of the contributor, his/her PERA contributions will be exempt from estate tax.
