# Determinant

A **determinant** of a matrix is a  function from $M_{n}(\mathbb{F})$ to $\mathbb{F}$.  It is used to determine the invertibility of a square matrix, solve a system of linear equationswith nonsingular coefficient matrix, and obtain scalars associated with a square matrix, called the eigenvalues of the matrix . 

We denote the determinant of matrix $A$ as $\text{det }A$ or $|A|$.
