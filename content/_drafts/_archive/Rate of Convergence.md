The **rate of convergence** describes how fast the sequence/method converges to the true solution.


---

>==**Definition.**== 
Suppose:
>- $\beta_n\to 0$ *a sequence that converges to 0*
>- $x_n\to x*$ a sequence $x_n$ that converges to the true solution $x*$
>
>We say $x_n\to x*$  with **rate of convergence** ("big Oh") $O(\beta_n)$ if there exists $C>0$ such that $$|x_n-x*|\leq C|\beta_n|$$ for large n.
