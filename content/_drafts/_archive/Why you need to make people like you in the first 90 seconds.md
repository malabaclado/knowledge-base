- Time has become an increasingly sought-after commodity. Yet time is one of the few tings we can't save - it's forever unfolding.
- In today's busy world, we instinctively assess other people. If we can't present ourselves fast and favorably, we run the risk of being politely, or impolitely, passed over. ^awqanq
- The reason for establishing likability within 90 seconds is because the attention span of an average person is only about 30 seconds. 
	- Attention craves novelty - it needs to be entertained and loves to leap from branch to branch. If there is nothing fresh and exciting for it to focus on, it becomes distracted and wanders off in search of something more compelling.
