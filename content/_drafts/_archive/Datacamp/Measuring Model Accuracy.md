# Measuring Model Accuracy

It is common practice to split your data into two sets, a training set and a test set. You train or fit the classifier on the training set. Then you make predictions on the labeled test set and compare these predictions with the known labels. You then compute the accuracy of your predictions. ^7c3bb8


Accuracy - # correct predictions / # total data points
