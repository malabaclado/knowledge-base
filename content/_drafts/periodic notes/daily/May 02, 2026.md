- [So Good They Can't Ignore You by Cal Newport | Summary | The Process Hacker](https://theprocesshacker.com/blog/so-good-they-cant-ignore-you-book-summary)
	- **The Craftsman Mindset**: an approach to life in which you focus on what value you can offer.
	- **Career Capital**: *"the traits that make a great job great are rare and valuable, and therefore if you want a great job, you need to build up rare and valuable skills to offer in return"*
	- **The Career Capital Theory of Great Work**: the key to work you love is not following your passion, but instead to get good at something rare and valuable, then cash in the Career Capital this generates to acquire the traits that define great jobs.
	- Basically, *what is something I can get really good at?*
	- **Deliberate Practice** – the style of difficult practice that requires you to stretch outside your comfort zone and then receive ruthless feedback on your performance”
		- This is basically **progressive overload**, applied to skills.
		- In career growth, many knowledge workers avoid developing relevant skills *because it is uncomfortable*. However, to build up large amounts of career capital, you should make deliberate practice a habitual part of your work routine.
		- **Stretch and Destroy**: Focus and stretch beyond you comfort zone.
