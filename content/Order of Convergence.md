> **Definition.** We say $x_n\to x*$ with **order of convergence** $\alpha$ if there exists $C>0$ such that $$\lim_{k\to \infty} \frac{|x_{k+1}-x*|}{|x_k-x*|^\alpha}=\lim_{k\to \infty} \frac{e_{k+1}}{{e_k}^\alpha}=C$$
> 
> When $\alpha=1$, we call it [[Linear Convergence]]. When $\alpha=2$, then [[Quadratic Convergence]].
